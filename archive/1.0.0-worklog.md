## changes

substep policy

- `src/core/config.js`: `substepsAuto:false` default (test mode), documented next to
  cflTarget/substepMax. `keyMap` gains `u`.
- `src/app/main.js`: ns is computed only when the policy is on; otherwise one step at
  `dt=frameDt`. the outflow limiter in compute_flux is unchanged and remains the safety net.
- `index.html` + `src/app/controls.js`: a "Substeps" checkbox, unchecked by default, synced
  both directions with cfg like the other toggles. `src/app/input.js`: `u` toggles it.

buoy

- new `src/core/buoy.js`: the body (position, velocity, held force), the six-float sample
  contract shared with the shader, the cpu sampler `sampleCpu`, and the spawn rule (dam only,
  16x64 reference rectangle scaled with N). no dom, no gpu, requires under node.
- `src/gpu/shaders.js`: `buoy_sample` (one 64-lane workgroup, workgroup reduction, six raw
  sums into a storage buffer) and the `buoyOverlay` block in `to_display`, which reads the
  same params slots the kernel does so what you see is what was sampled.
- `src/gpu/fields.js`: `pool.buoy` (storage) + `pool.buoyRead` (MAP_READ), destroyed with the pool.
- `src/gpu/passes.js`: pipeline, four-entry bind group per (mass, vel) parity, `encodeBuoySample`.
- `src/gpu/solver.js`: owns the body (so reset/resize respawn it), marches it once per frame
  before writeParams, requests a sample every `buoySampleEvery` frames, copies 24 bytes back in
  the same submit, and ingests it in `pollReadback` (renamed from `pollStats`, which is what it
  became). a stale-generation guard, same as stats, so a readback that lands after a reset is dropped.
- `src/app/{main,input,controls}.js`, `index.html`: `y` key and a "Buoy" checkbox; toggling
  respawns the body instead of resetting the sim. hud shows `buoy y=… vy=… submerged=…%`
  in the state block and in the "Copy stats" snapshot.
- nothing in `src/core/solver.js` or the sim kernels changed: the body is a reader.

tests and experiments

- `experiments/conformance.js`: `testSubstepPolicy`, `testBuoy`, `testBuoyModel`; the harness
  gained a shared `substepCount(cfg,maxVel)` mirroring main.js and an `onFrameEnd` hook so a
  test can drive a second module alongside the sim. makeCfg now turns the policy on explicitly.
- new `experiments/buoy.js`: one fluid run, 14 body variants. `--n` (grid), `--frames`,
  `--every`, `--scenario`, `--coarse=1` to skip the second grid. prints the rest submersion and
  its band, end cy/vy, max and mean per-frame motion, `settled@f` and a 50-frame float trace.
- new `experiments/gpu-mock.js`: recording mock device + encoder, real `Fluid.gpu.solver`
  frames, headless. kept separate from conformance because it tests the gpu plumbing, not the
  scheme, and it can never be a numeric oracle (nothing executes the wgsl).

## decisions and caveats

- read-only by design (user: "rather than building it into physics solver"). the ledger,
  the drift meters and every field are untouched; a displacing body needs a moving wall mask
  and swept-volume accounting, which is its own plan.
- the lift is the blue tracer per cell area inside the rectangle, exactly as briefed. with
  more dye per cell than `buoyRho` the lift saturates and the body can ride spray and smeared
  dye (blue x2 ends above the water); `buoyRho` is the tuning knob, noted in the plan.
- submersion is reported against the *local peak* blue inside the footprint, so the meter reads
  half submerged whether the scenario carries b=0.1 or b=1.
- `buoyGain` scales lift and weight together: a stiffer spring, the same float line.
- the rectangle is a block of whole cells; `round(center - size/2)` is the one origin that the
  kernel, the sampler and the overlay share, so no half-cell disagreement between them.
- the app default (policy off) makes the demo less accurate out of the box; that is what was
  asked for testing, and the checkbox plus `u` turn it back on. if it annoys in normal use,
  flip `substepsAuto` in config defaults and the conformance makeCfg stays as it is.
