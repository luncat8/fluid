## changes

- core/solver.js: deriveT (therm.g is now a cache of E/(m*c), T_amb in vacuum,
  refreshed after transport and after sources, and after dye patches);
  conduction as clamped pairwise exchange into the ping-pong buffer (never in
  place), wallCool exchange against wall/domain-edge neighbours charged to the
  ledger; heatSources (bottom-band fixed power, top-band cold plate toward
  T_amb). Step order: … advectScalar, deriveT, conduction, heatSources, deriveT.
  therm.b/a repurposed from a constant 1/0 to cumulative injected/removed
  source energy per cell: donor transport carries them, so their field totals
  are the source ledger on both cpu (summed directly) and gpu (stats readback).
- scenario "convection": full box, m=1, sinusoidal T seed, thermalSources on.
  It joins the scenario selector/key cycle automatically via scenarioNames.
- core/stats.js: ledger heatInE/heatOutE in expectedTotal("E"); thermalTotals
  (T range/mean over cells with substance, kinetic from face-averaged cell
  velocities — the same reconstruction in the WGSL reduction, indices 17–22).
- gpu: deriveT/conduction/heatSources shaders and dispatch chain in
  passes.encodeStep (mass slot keyed to the post-FCT slot), stats reduction
  extended (source sums, T range/mean, kinetic), mode 6 signed thermal view,
  solver readback folds the source-sum delta into the expected E of the
  snapshot it belongs to. HUD gained a T/heat/KE line.
- tuning: beta 4e-4 -> 2e-3 (only bites where T varies, i.e. nowhere before
  this plan), heatPower 30, coolTop 0.5, heatBand 2, conductivity 0.2 kept.

## validation

`experiments/logs/2026-10-19-convection.log` records experiments/convection.js:
- derive_T exact at 350 K, vacuum reads T_amb.
- conduction: E drift 0.0 over 4000 exchanges; hot/cold halves relax to
  308.2..311.8 around the 310 mean with no overshoot; conductivity=1e9 hits
  the clamp (1984 faces) and stays monotone.
- sources: 600 steps gravity-off, E drift 1.5e-7, stored E + kinetic closure
  1.5e-7 (the plan's 1e-3 bar, measured without gravity work — see plan t6).
- convection: onset (maxVel > 2) at 1.75 s, peak 8.4 cells/s, hot cells rise
  on average (+0.036), bottom quarter 312.6 K vs top 292.4 K after 5000 steps.
- stability: 10x beta and 10x conductivity stay finite, no negative cells.

Also re-run: conformance (all pass, new thermal passes in every step),
gpu-mock (all pass, params-slot lint covers the new indices), input/controls/ui
contracts (28/17/22 pass), node --check on all JS.

Two measured floors worth knowing:
- mass drift in the 5000-step convection run is 1.1e-5 and grows roughly
  linearly (~2e-9/step) under sustained boundary jets, while E stays at
  3.1e-7. With conduction off the same run lands at 4e-8, so this is f32
  field-store rounding in the velocity realisation, not a thermal leak.
  Watch it if a future plan needs tighter long-run mass budgets.
- conduction at the plan formula (coef*dT*dt against capacity m*c) is slow:
  diffusion time over L cells is L^2*C/coef, ~5000 s across 32 cells at the
  0.2 default. Convection carries the heat here; conduction smooths locally.
