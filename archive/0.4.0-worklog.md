## changes

- core/solver.js: deriveT (therm.g is now a cache of E/(m*c), T_amb in vacuum,
  refreshed after transport and after sources, and after dye patches);
  conduction as clamped pairwise exchange into the ping-pong buffer (never in
  place), wallCool exchange against wall/domain-edge neighbours charged to the
  ledger; heatSources (bottom-band fixed power, top-band cold plate toward
  T_amb). Step order: … advectScalar, deriveT, conduction, heatSources, deriveT.
  therm.b/a repurposed from a constant 1/0 to cumulative injected/removed
  source energy per cell: donor transport carries them, so their field totals
  are the source ledger on both cpu (summed directly) and gpu (stats readback).
- scenario "convection": full box, m=1, sinusoidal T seed, thermalSources on.
  It joins the scenario selector/key cycle automatically via scenarioNames.
- core/stats.js: ledger heatInE/heatOutE in expectedTotal("E"); thermalTotals
  (T range/mean over cells with substance, kinetic from face-averaged cell
  velocities — the same reconstruction in the WGSL reduction, indices 17–22).
- gpu: deriveT/conduction/heatSources shaders and dispatch chain in
  passes.encodeStep (mass slot keyed to the post-FCT slot), stats reduction
  extended (source sums, T range/mean, kinetic), mode 6 signed thermal view,
  solver readback folds the source-sum delta into the expected E of the
  snapshot it belongs to. HUD gained a T/heat/KE line.
- tuning: beta 4e-4 -> 2e-3 (only bites where T varies, i.e. nowhere before
  this plan), heatPower 30, coolTop 0.5, heatBand 2, conductivity 0.2 kept.

## validation

`experiments/logs/2026-10-19-convection.log` records experiments/convection.js:
- derive_T exact at 350 K, vacuum reads T_amb.
- conduction: after matching WGSL's f32 operation rounding, E drift is
  2.34e-8 over 4000 exchanges (within the 1e-7 f32 floor); hot/cold halves
  relax to 308.2..311.8 around the 310 mean with no overshoot; conductivity=1e9
  hits the clamp (1984 faces) and stays monotone.
- sources: 600 steps gravity-off, E drift and stored E + kinetic closure are
  both 6.48e-8 (the plan's 1e-3 bar, measured without gravity work — see t6).
- convection: onset (maxVel > 2) at 1.75 s, peak 8.32 cells/s, hot cells rise
  on average (+0.037), bottom quarter 312.6 K vs top 292.4 K after 5000 steps.
- stability: 10x beta and 10x conductivity stay finite, no negative cells.

Also re-run: conformance (all pass, new thermal passes in every step),
gpu-mock (all pass, params-slot lint covers the new indices), input/controls/ui
contracts (28/17/22 pass), node --check on all JS.

Two measured floors worth knowing:
- the current 5000-step convection run has mass drift 4.67e-8 and E drift
  4.07e-7. The earlier 1.1e-5 mass figure was from before the f32 thermal
  arithmetic alignment and is superseded by this run.
- conduction at the plan formula (coef*dT*dt against capacity m*c) is slow:
  diffusion time over L cells is L^2*C/coef, ~5000 s across 32 cells at the
  0.2 default. Convection carries the heat here; conduction smooths locally.

## 2026-10-19 parity follow-up

- `config.writeParams()` omitted `rhoMean` at params slot 27 even though the GPU
  force kernel reads it. The GPU therefore applied the wrong mass-buoyancy force;
  writing the live mean fixes dam parity, and `gpu-mock.js` now checks the f32 slot.
- The CPU pressure projection had Jacobi sweeps while WGSL uses red-black Gauss-Seidel.
  CPU `project()` now mirrors the GPU's two color passes; the closed-box checkerboard
  regression removes the mode in one sweep. GPU mock checks that the WGSL color gate
  and opposite-color copy remain present.
- CPU thermal operations now round key intermediates to f32 to better match WGSL.
  This exposes a small expected f32 floor in conduction: relative E drift 2.34e-8
  over 4000 exchanges; the test accepts below 1e-7, still well below parity tolerances.
- `experiments/gpu-parity.mjs` now runs actual compute through Node `webgpu`/Dawn on
  the SwiftShader Vulkan backend, reading back mass, therm, velocity, pressure, flux,
  divergence source, and flags at expanded heated-box snapshots. The app itself gains
  no runtime dependency. The log is `experiments/logs/2026-10-19-gpu-parity.log`.
- N=32, dam flow steps 1–8, and all 500 heated-box steps complete without shader
  validation errors. At heat step 500, aggregate total-E and mean-T relative errors
  are 9.37e-9 and 7.91e-9. Pointwise max-relative E/T errors are 3.73e-5 and
  3.72e-5 (RMS 8.98e-3 and 2.14e-3 absolute); at step 64 they are already
  2.69e-6 and 2.72e-6, then grow to about 5.9e-6 at 128 and 1.3e-5 at 256.
  The runner reports both, gates aggregate metrics at 2e-6, and permits a 5e-5
  pointwise f32 bound after step 64. The user clarified that t8 means aggregate
  total-E/mean-T, so the measured run meets the chosen acceptance. Pointwise drift
  remains visible in the log as a stricter diagnostic, not a t8 gate.
- Final CPU checks pass: conformance (including checkerboard, buoy, and all-channel
  emitter checks), convection, GPU mock, and buoy study. The buoy test now samples
  through 500 frames so it observes the down/up cycle rather than ending at its
  transient floor-contact point. `experiments/buoy.js` also shows resolution
  sensitivity: the default body is about 47.6% submerged at N=64/f=600, but 11.3%
  at N=128/f=400; this is logged, not altered as part of t8.
