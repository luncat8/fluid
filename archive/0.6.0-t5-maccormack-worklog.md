## what changed

- `src/core/solver.js`: the two bilinear samplers and the trace-velocity average that
  `advectVel` inlined are now `sampleU`/`sampleV`/`traceU`/`traceV`, so both schemes read
  one definition of the staggered tap. `advectVel` is bit-identical after the refactor
  (below). `advectVelMac` is the second scheme: pass 1 is the same semi-lagrangian trace
  into a scratch field, pass 2 traces that field forward along the same face velocity and
  corrects with `c = f + (src - back)/2`, limited into the min/max of the four taps pass 1
  used. `advectVelocity(dt)` is the single entry point `step()` and the oracles call.
- `diag.macLimit` (faces the limiter moved) and `diag.macClamp` (vClamp hits) are the
  watchdog, cleared in `reset()` and reported by `stats()`.
- `src/core/config.js`: `advectMac: 0`, `macLimiter: 1`. CPU only — there is no uniform
  slot, because a slot the wgsl does not read is a lie.
- `experiments/vortex-decay.js` + `run_vortex_decay.py`: the t5 decision metric. Gaussian
  vortex, rim 80 cells/s, sigma=N/6, gravity and heat off, viscosity 0, dt=1/240, so the
  0.33 cell per step displacement is the auto policy's at 60 fps and every joule lost is
  numerical diffusion. The loop is velocity only (mass stays uniform, so the projection is
  constant coefficient) and uses the shipping projection by default; `--no-proj` is the
  attribution row. All sampling is allocation free: one `measure()` pass writes a caller
  owned object, and the mac scratch, stencil and counters are allocated once.

## measurement (N=128, 960 steps = 4 s)

| | sl | mac (limited) | mac raw |
|---|---|---|---|
| KE kept at 1 s | 0.8381 | 0.9627 | 0.9627 |
| KE kept at 4 s | 0.5628 | 0.8742 | 0.8742 |
| peak vorticity kept at 4 s | 0.5960 | 0.9044 | 0.9044 |
| enstrophy kept at 4 s | 0.4853 | 0.8320 | 0.8320 |
| maxDiv at 4 s | 5.04e-3 | 3.20e-3 | 3.66e-3 |
| peak speed over the run | 80.00 | 80.00 | 80.00 |
| vClamp watchdog | — | 0 | 0 |
| cpu ms/step (advection slice) | 52.42 (4.46) | 63.94 (12.38) | 61.86 (11.48) |

- mac/sl retained KE is 1.149x at 1 s and 1.553x at 4 s; retained peak vorticity is
  1.517x. The quality bar (>= 1.25x) is cleared at 4 s and cannot be reached at 1 s by
  any scheme: the ceiling is 1/keep and sl has only lost 16 % by then, so the ceiling is
  1.193x. The bar has to name the vortex age; the plan now does.
- The limiter is mandatory, and `--no-proj` is where that is visible. Raw mac-cormack
  without a projection creates energy: peak speed 80 -> 105 -> 270 -> 400 -> 441 over
  4 s, retained KE 1.31, peak vorticity 198x, vClamp tripped 256 times, maxDiv 2.5e5.
  Limited, the peak speed never leaves 80.00 and vClamp stays 0 (the limiter moved 1.15e6
  faces over the 960 steps, 1624 in the worst one). The mechanism is the wall face: a backward
  trace from the face next to a blocked face reads that face's 0, so the raw correction
  extrapolates 7.7 % past the boundary value every step and it compounds (the
  constant-field test prints it). With the projection on, the solve removes that overshoot
  each step, which is why limited and raw agree to six digits here — and why the limiter
  cannot be dropped just because the product path has a projection.
- Cost: +22 % on the cpu step (wall clock, a few percent of run variance), but the
  advection slice is +178 % and is only 9 % of that step; the rest is dominated by the
  projection. On the device the vel group is 0.033 ms of a 1.448 ms substep sum (2 %,
  `05_55` jet), so the frame-time bar cannot be read off the cpu number in either
  direction.

## decision

Not adopted. The quality half of plan 4.2 clears at 4 s and the watchdog is 0, but the
15 % half is a device number and there is no wgsl kernel. `advectMac` stays 0 and the
shipping path is unchanged. t16 is the port plus the device row; carrying
(u, v, traceU, traceV) in one rgba32float scratch lets pass 2 do 4 texture taps instead
of 8, which is where the cpu +172 % lives.

## validation

- The `advectVel` refactor is free: `node experiments/residual-spike.js` reproduces
  `2026-10-21-residual-spike.log` to the printed digits (weight jump 1.000e+3 -> 4.995e+5,
  melt 0 -> 2.000e+5, dam N=64 worst end/solve 5.56e+2 at step 10, solve/rhs 1.356e-4).
- The fixed-point check in the oracle asserts what the scheme must do on a field with no
  curvature: interior faces are bit-identical between sl and mac (diff 0.00e+0) and both
  preserve the constant exactly, so the correction has no bias; the wall band shows the
  limited overshoot at 0 and the raw one at 7.7 % per step.
- `node experiments/run-all.js --quick`: 12/12. `node experiments/run-all.js`: 19/19 in
  1043 s with the refactor in place (conformance, phase, solid-growth, buoy, multigrid and
  the gpu mock all read the samplers through `advectVelocity`).
- `experiments/vortex-decay.js` is added to the full profile: it carries the advection
  invariants (constant-field fixed point, boundedness, vClamp watchdog) that nothing else
  in the suite asserts, and it fails the run if a sampler regression breaks them.
- Boundedness is asserted per run, not just printed: the limited scheme's peak speed over
  every checkpoint must not exceed its initial value, and vClamp must stay 0.

## next

t16 (wgsl mac-cormack + the device frame-time row) is the only thing that can adopt t5,
and it should be measured against the same vortex on the device rather than against the
cpu oracle. Cheaper candidates that are still open and do not need a new kernel: t8 pass
fusion (derive_T into conduction, heat_sources into phase_eos) and t13 (the readback ring
and the display-size trial). t6 vorticity confinement is the other quality lever for
"soft vortices" and is a force, so it can be measured on the same oracle by adding a
confinement row instead of a scheme row.
