# 0.7.5 — the fast disc across ice, reproduced headlessly

date: 2026-11-10
status: **root cause narrowed, no fix shipped.** the failure now reproduces deterministically in
about 35 s, four candidate cures were measured and rejected, and the shape of the anomaly is
pinned. see "what this does not do".
plan: none yet (this round is diagnosis; a fix plan follows from §"next")
source: the user's device captures in the 0.7.4 session — three HUD states, broken leg at
`nonFinite=63692`, `maxM=30791.291`, `maxVel=0 maxDiv=0`, probes reading `—`, with the
trigger described as *"if collider very fast move across ice"*

## what this round establishes

**the failure is real, is not a driver or wgsl artefact, and is now reproducible on the cpu twin
in 35 seconds.** that single fact changes the problem: 0.7.4 could only say "the device died
somewhere between two frames and no harness in the repo ever reproduced it". the six-emitter rig
plus a fast drag disc across `ice` does reproduce it, deterministically, at N=64 as well as
N=128.

the earlier 0.7.4 conclusion that "whatever breaks is in wgsl or in the driver" is **withdrawn**.
it was a statement about the harnesses that existed then, not about the solver.

## the reproducer

`experiments/collider-ice.js` — six-emitter rig from `examples/demo.js`, scenario `ice`, a circle
collider dragged on a Lissajous orbit, `substepCount` and `velocityCap` driven from live stats,
and a pass-by-pass bisect over all fourteen entries of `step()` (including `updateColliderMask`
and `imprintColliders`; omitting either changes the physics and hides the bug).

	# the n=128 row the device showed, ~5 min, dies at frame 218-219

`STRESS=1` tightens and speeds the orbit (`0.21/0.17` vs `0.09/0.13`, amplitude `0.45/0.40` vs
`0.42/0.34`). the gentle path needs N=128 and ~220 frames; the stress path reaches the same
failure at N=64 inside the budget a ci row can afford.

deterministic: same frame, same substep, same pass, across repeated runs and both grid sizes.

## what the substep trace shows

instrumented trace of frames 91-92 (`/tmp/watch.js`, reproduced below in §"evidence"):

- the failing cells are on the **`y=1` edge row** (`20,64` then `47,6` then `27,1`, `29,1`,
  `40,2`, `30,2`, `31,1`, `19,1`, `25,1`). the ghost ring is 0 there, and `isBlocked` returns
  true for `y<1`, so the edge row is the one place where a clamp can read a zero pressure.
- mass on the failing cell swings **thousands of times between substeps**: `0.70 -> 20.5 -> 6.7
  -> 8.7` at frame 91, and `59.3 -> 12.2 -> 0.01` at frame 92. this is the 1/rho face weight the
  pressure operator uses.
- the solve residual is **5-6 orders of magnitude above the rhs it is solving** while the field
  is still finite: `poissonRes 6.91e+11` against `poissonRhs 8.74e+5` at frame 91 substep 7;
  `1.39e+29` against `2.38e+6` at frame 92 substep 5.
- pressure amplifies by a steady ~10x **per substep, every substep**, once the runaway starts:
  `-1.5e+16 -> -8.8e+17 -> 9.9e+18 -> 2.0e+20 -> -1.7e+22 -> -6.1e+24 -> -3.4e+27 -> -2.0e+30`.
  that is not a slow divergence, it is a repeated amplification with a fixed factor.
- velocity is pinned at the clamp or near it (`378`, `480`) throughout, so the *velocity* field is
  not the thing running away first — the pressure is.

the divisor is `1/(2 ra rb/(ra+rb))` with `ra, rb` the mass densities of the two cells, floored at
`rhoVac=1e-3`. a cell at `m=0.01` gives a face weight of ~200x a cell at `m=1`, and a cell at
`4e-12` gives ~1e9x before the floor applies.

## candidate cures measured and rejected

each was implemented, run against the reproducer, and reverted. none is in the tree.

	change                                   result                          verdict
	---------------------------------------  ------------------------------  ------------------
	applyGrad clamp to vClamp (cpu + wgsl)   dies at 219 instead of 218      not a fix
	                                          (one substep later)
	global pressure gauge after project      dies at 248 instead of 218      not a fix
	per-component (flood-fill) gauge         dies at 78                      not a fix
	divergence masked by the operator's      dies at 16-30 (much worse)      wrong direction
	  own face mask in poissonRhs
	rhs mean subtraction (compatibility      dies at 248                     not a fix
	  projection)
	density floor 0.02 / 0.08 / 0.3 / 0.5    dies at 21 / 20 / 20 / 22       wrong direction
	  applied to faceW + faceInvRho + mg

two notes that matter for whoever picks this up:

- the `applyGrad` clamp is the answer to a *different* question. it is still arguably correct —
  `applyGrad` is the only velocity write in the solver with no bound at all, while
  `imprintColliders`, the advector and `computeFlux` all clamp to `vClamp` — but it moves the
  death by one substep, so it is not *the* fix and shipping it as one would be the exact mistake
  0.7.2 made. it was reverted rather than kept, to keep the tree honest about what is proven.
- the density floor result is the important negative. it makes things **four times worse**, which
  rules out the simplest reading of the 1/rho story: the runaway is not "the floor is too low".
  an attempt to floor `faceW` *without* `faceInvRho` and the multigrid `faceWeight` was also
  measured and discarded as invalid — flooring one of the three breaks v-cycle consistency and
  measures nothing.

## dead ends, so they are not retried

- sealed/inconsistent connected components: not the cause. a flood-fill component gauge (each
  open component's own mean pressure removed) changed nothing (dies at 78 either way), so there is
  no isolated pocket holding a runaway datum.
- the collider *shape* and *speed* are not the axis: a rect collider dies at the same frame 218 as
  a circle at 480 u/s, and a circle at 200 u/s dies *earlier* (frame 130). the trigger is the
  ice phase changing density under the disc, not the disc.
- the drain emitter is not the cause: the rig with all five other emitters and no drain dies at
  the same frame 218.
- `rhsSum` over open cells is nonzero in the healthy phase too (`-3e2 .. -5e3`), so a
  compatibility violation alone does not explain death; it is a second, separate observation.
- statistics readouts go blind at the moment of death: `poissonRes`, `divBefore` and `rhsSum` read
  0 / stale once `captureSolveResidual` runs on a non-finite field, so a harness must not gate on
  them after the field is dead (this cost one false "FAIL" in the reproducer's own anomaly check,
  which is now printed rather than asserted).

## what this does not do

**no fix.** the root cause is narrowed to "the pressure solve amplifies every substep once the ice
phase collapses density under the disc, with a residual 5-6 orders above the source, on the edge
row", and four plausible cures are measured and rejected. the remaining unexplained fact is the
fixed ~10x-per-substep amplification factor, which smells like an iteration-gain problem in
`project` — possibly `mgPost=8` / `mgCycles=2` under-converging while `poissonRes` is enormous, or
a genuine instability in the Gauss-Seidel sweep when a face weight spans 1e9.

not attempted this round, in rough order of what the evidence points at:

1. **solve convergence under the runaway.** `poissonRes` 1e11 against rhs 9e5 means the solve is
   diverging, not merely inaccurate. check whether the v-cycle is stable when face weights span
   many decades, and whether `mgPre/mgPost/mgCycles` are adequate at `ns=24`. this is the
   best-supported lead.
2. **the edge row.** every failing cell is on `y=1`. the ghost ring is 0 and `isBlocked` treats
   `y<1` as wall, so the edge row is where a clamp reads zero pressure. worth asking why the
   runaway always starts there and never in the interior.
3. **where the mass swing comes from.** `59.3 -> 0.01` in one substep on an open cell is not
   physical; it is the input to the 1/rho weight. if the mass swing is itself a bug, the pressure
   story is downstream.
4. the `applyGrad` clamp, gauge fix and density floor remain *available* but are not fixes; do not
   re-ship them as such.
