# 0.7.4 — a non-finite field is no longer silent

date: 2026-11-10
status: implemented, validated headlessly (quick suite 13/13, full 21/21, wgsl-exec); device row open
plan: 0.7.4-plan.md
source: the 0.7.3 device capture in `archive/0.7.3-worklog.md`, where the demo page reported a
healthy field while the probes read `—`

## what the capture actually shows

two of the three 0.7.3 snapshots contain a field that is already dead, and nothing on the page
says so. the tell is the probe table:

	probe 0   205,191  m 0.302  T 293.0 K  liquid   u,v,p = —
	probe 1   104,25   m 0.039  T 373.0 K  boiling  u,v,p = —

`—` is `fmt()`'s non-finite branch (`examples/demo.js`, `src/app/hud.js`). the cells hold a
finite mass and a finite temperature, and NaN velocity — while the meters directly under them,
read from the same readback, said `maxVel=0 maxDiv=0` and `maxM=1.001`.

## why every meter said nothing

- **gpu.** `statsReduceA` seeds `maxVel`, `maxDiv`, `pMin`, `pMax` and folds them with WGSL
  `max()`/`min()`. `max(x, NaN)` returns `x` in WGSL, so a NaN velocity or pressure anywhere in
  the field is discarded by the reduction and every derived meter reads calm. `maxM` looks small
  because the cell that swallowed the mass is the NaN one. the poisson residual looks *better*
  than healthy (16.7 -> 0.19) because NaN cells fail `poissonOpen`'s early return.
- **cpu oracle.** `totalsFromFields` / `motionTotals` compare with `<`/`>`, and every comparison
  against NaN is false, so a NaN cell updates no extremum and no counter. the same blind spot,
  reached a different way.
- the conservation totals were the one place a NaN should have shown up (`E` includes
  `0.5*m*(u²+v²)`), and `drift=-8.2e-9 [ok]` was reported instead. that is the second, separate
  question: whether the kahan total in `statsReduceB` can be silently dropped as well. it is
  listed under "next" and not claimed here.

so the field died somewhere between frame#691 and #781 — visible as `maxDiv 28.6`, `over 457`
then `over 109`, `maxM 27084` — and the page kept rendering numbers that looked plausible for
another twenty seconds. that is what cost three rounds: 0.7.1 was recorded "then broken",
0.7.3 reproduced it, and neither capture can say which frame died or which field died first.

## implementation

five counters in the five free floats of the existing stats slot.

	slot  name              counts
	46    IDX_NONFINITE     cells with any group set (the headline)
	47    IDX_NF_MASS       m, dr, dg, db
	48    IDX_NF_THERM      E, T
	49    IDX_NF_VEL        u, v
	50    IDX_NF_PRESS      p

- `src/core/stats.js`: `STATS_LEN` 46 -> 51, still one 256-byte aligned slot (`51*4 = 204 B`),
  so the readback layout, the one-map rule and the 40-float partial stride are untouched. tests
  are written `!(x === x)` because that is the one thing `<`/`>` cannot express.
  `packStatsArray` writes all five; `nonFiniteTotal()` is exported.
- `src/gpu/shaders.js`: `statsReduceA` gained four 0/1 per-cell flags reduced with `+` in the
  same stride loop (shared array 2240 -> 2560 B), landing in the free tail of each partial
  (slots 35..38 of 40); `statsReduceB` sums them into 47..50 and writes the worst group into 46.
  one pass, one buffer, one dispatch, no new copy.
- `src/gpu/solver.js`: `ingestStats` reads 46..50 into `statsCache.cntNonFinite` and
  `statsCache.nonFinite={mass,therm,vel,press}`; `getStats()` exposes both plus
  `firstNonFiniteFrame`, a latch that records the first stats frame that left zero and is
  cleared by `reset()`/`resize()`. runtime state, never serialized — the same rule the buoy
  `dP/dt` baseline follows.
- `src/api/engine.js`: `copyStats` carries all five plus the latch onto the game-facing view.
  without this the counters reach `sim.solver.getStats()` and stop there, which is exactly how
  a diagnostic gets declared done and then read as zero.
- `src/app/hud.js`: the health line now *leads* with
  `NON-FINITE FIELD: N cells (m=.. T=.. u,v=.. p=..) first seen frame#..`, and
  `buildSnapshot` prints `nonFinite=N (...) firstNonFiniteFrame#..` on the max line so a pasted
  report carries it with the health line stripped.
- `examples/demo.js`: the conservation meter gained `nonFinite=N` beside `neg=`/`over=`.
- `README.md`: one paragraph on why this counter exists.

## what this does not do

**the divergence is not fixed.** the root cause is not identified. the cpu oracle was driven
through every path that is reachable headlessly and stayed clean: the demo's six-emitter rig on
`dam` for 1200 frames at N=64 and 900 frames at N=160 (no non-finite cell at any pass boundary,
checked after each of the eleven passes), collider drag at 480 u/s for 500 frames, `cycle` with
melt/boil, `freeze` and `drain`. so whatever breaks is in wgsl or in the driver, and no harness in
this repo executes wgsl (wgsl-exec parses and interprets individual kernels, it cannot run the
frame). that is why this round ships the *counter* and not a fix: the next device capture names
the frame and the field, which is the input the search needs.

two candidate mechanisms were read and left alone because the evidence does not choose between
them: (a) `poissonFaceW`/`faceW`/`invRho` divide by `(ra + rb)` with `ra, rb >= rhoVac`, which is
safe for finite inputs but maps a NaN density straight to a NaN gradient across the whole
neighbourhood; (b) the WGSL `forces` kernel's `evalScalarCell` freeze branch and `EOS_HELPERS`
`rhoStar=1/vStar` both divide by mass-derived quantities, and a NaN there would propagate the same
way. both want a `firstNonFiniteFrame` from a device before they are worth pursuing.
