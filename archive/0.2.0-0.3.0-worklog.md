## 2026-10-18 — draw blowup: per-cell outflow limiter in compute_flux (post-0.3.0 fix)

Review of the previous task ("fix draw & physics 'bad' drift"): encodePaint
dispatches and parity handling are sound (paintMass/paintTherm read m / write
1-m together, OOB workgroup threads are covered by webgpu robust access); the
ledger-injection accounting is exact (user dump: exp m/dr grew by the painted
1576.006 in lockstep). The reported draw crash was not in the paint path.

User report: healthy at frame#445 (maxM=0.53, all invariants [ok]); "few
seconds after draw" the totals are NaN, maxM=2.29e38, neg=3479, over=176,
p[0..0], maxVel=0. Reproduced on the cpu oracle at N=256 with a scripted dye
stroke + drag stir and the app's own substep rule (ns from statsEvery-stale
maxVel): sumM went NaN 50 frames into the stroke. Frame probe:

  f=160  first cells with k_out > 1 (140 cells, maxOut 1.2)
  f=168  first negative cells (minM 0.11 -> -8 next frame)
  f=169+ maxOut saturates at 4.0 (all four faces clamped to +-1);
         maxM/minM multiply by ~3-40 per frame (the q'=q*(1-k)+in update
         with k=4 gives factor |1-k|=3 on the sign-alternating mode)
  f=177  f32 totals lose cancellation (sumM lurches), f=186 sumM NaN

Root cause: the +-1 per-face flux clamp is not a monotonicity guarantee. Donor
cell needs the per-cell total outflow k_out <= 1; a diverging cell (draw:
overpack expansion S + drag stir + stale-substep CFL spikes) can empty through
up to 4 faces at once and the transport amplifies. The plan's "cfl |u|dt <= 1
per axis" claim was wrong for divergent flow even at the clamp.

Fix (0.0.0-plan.md sections 5.1/5.2 amended to match):

- compute_flux now scales the faces a cell owns as outflow so they sum to <= 1
  volume per step. Each face leaves exactly one of its two cells (sign of the
  flux decides), so the scale factor is unique per face and the stored flux
  stays the single shared value both neighbours read with opposite signs:
  conservation is untouched, monotonicity is unconditional. Same limiter in
  src/core/solver.js and the compute_flux wgsl (helpers rawFx/rawFy/outFlux,
  owner selected by flux sign).
- app/main.js substep estimate now includes the live drag speed
  (max(stale maxVel, |mouseVX|, |mouseVY|)): the readback is up to 30 frames
  stale and the draw was the trigger. The limiter remains the hard safety net.
