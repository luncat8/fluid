## review of the previous task and the supplied GPU capture

The capture closes the last open 0.5.0 item. It ran the five phase scenarios on the
target hardware (nvidia ampere, browser WebGPU) at N=512, 600 frames each, MG
solver (`mgS4x2(pre4/post8)`), K=60, auto substeps, FCT on, served with COOP/COEP
so the timer is fine (`crossOriginIsolated=true · observed quantum=0.005ms`,
unlike the 0.1 ms clamp of the first capture). The user also confirms the
interactive page looks correct on the same device (views + solid overlay), so the
visual side of a8/a9 holds on real hardware, complementing the Dawn/Vulkan
parity readbacks. Results, worst case last:

| scenario | frames/s | speed | queue ms/frame | final ns | ~ms/substep |
|----------|----------|-------|----------------|----------|-------------|
| ice      | 133.5    | 2.23x | 7.33           | 3/24     | 2.44        |
| rain     | 67.9     | 1.13x | 14.52          | 7/24     | 2.07        |
| arch     | 46.8     | 0.78x | 21.14          | 8/24     | 2.64        |
| cycle    | 43.3     | 0.72x | 22.81          | 15/24    | ~2.3 (est.) |
| jet      | 33.3     | 0.55x | 29.77          | 12/24    | 2.48        |

(cycle's final-frame ns=15 exceeds its run average — boiling intensifies over the
600 frames — so its per-substep figure is back-computed from the 23.1 ms average
frame; all five land at ~2.1-2.6 ms/substep. Queue wait is the serial,
scheduling-inclusive wait, an upper bound on GPU time, not a GPU timestamp.)

Verdicts against 0.5.0 acceptance:

- a10 at N=512 — pass. Worst case `jet` at 33.3 fps >= 30 fps with the phase
  passes included. The frame rate is substep-count bound: CPU encode is only
  0.13-0.29 ms/frame, everything else is GPU queue wait, and the boiling
  scenarios pay for ns=12-15 (CFL bound 0.67-0.90, so auto substeps keep margin).
- a10 at N=256 — not captured on hardware; closed by scaling, not measurement.
  Per-frame work scales as ns x cells: at N=256 cells drop 4x and the CFL dt
  doubles (h doubles), so frame cost drops ~8x — `jet` ≈ 3.7 ms ≈ 270 fps.
  Even with ns held at 12 it is ~139 fps, >2x the 60 fps bar. N=512 is also the
  shipping default (`defaults.N`), so 512 was the binding clause. A one-command
  confirmation capture (`n=256&frames=600&scenarios=jet,cycle`) would make this
  a measurement; it is optional and does not block 0.6.0.
- a7/a11 on hardware — `neg=0` and `sSat=0` in all five scenarios, with
  90k-108k boiling cells in cycle/jet/rain. The S saturation watchdog stays
  silent under violent boiling at the chosen defaults.
- conservation — worst |drift| is 3.3e-6 (E in cycle). The four `[warn]` flags
  (m in ice, E in cycle/jet/rain) sit just above the 1e-6 "ok" line, ~1500x
  below the 5e-3 closure budget of a4: f32 summation noise over 600 frames x
  up to 15 substeps, not leaks (the flux-pair scheme is exactly conservative by
  construction; the residual is in the totals readout).
- reported-not-gated peaks are physical: sampled maxDiv 29-42 in the boiling
  scenarios is the S expansion source itself, while liquid-only ice/arch sit at
  0.019/0.065 (final frame). Worst transient over-compression is maxM 1.056 in
  arch (sampled), 1.003 final in cycle. jet's p band [1.400e6..1.407e6] is the
  EOS absolute offset (relative variation ~0.5 %).

One oddity was investigated before signing off: `fMom=0` in all five scenarios,
including `rain` with 42436 solid cells. It is genuine, not a dead counter.
`frozenMomentum` is charged only on the freeze *transition* (`frozen &&
prevFl.b == 0`), and a CPU-oracle probe (rain/cycle, N=128, dt=1/240, 2400 steps
= the same 10 s of sim time) shows every transition happens at init while
velocity is exactly 0; the solid inventories only shrink afterwards
(solCells 2688 -> 2595 in rain, 954 -> 946 in cycle, zero new transitions).
The N=512 counts agree by arithmetic: rain ends with 42436 solid cells vs
83 initialized cold rows x 512 = 42496; cycle with 13192 vs a 205x67 = 13735
initial ice block. All five scenarios net-melt or hold — none freezes a moving
stream — so the correct value is 0. Residual gap, noted not blocking: the GPU
fMom path has never carried a nonzero value end-to-end (gpu-parity.mjs compares
phaseCounts and latentChargedE, not frozenMomentum). The CPU synthetic test
(phase.js freeze flip) proves the charging logic, the WGSL mirrors it
line-for-line, and latE travels the same accum texture + reduction path and
reads back correctly (1.7e8-2.6e8 where boiling is heavy). A future
freeze-from-motion scenario (e.g. supercooled jet onto a cold wall) would be
the first live exercise of the counter.

## next

0.6.0 solver/substep performance tuning now has a hardware baseline to beat:
~2.1-2.6 ms per substep at 512^2 (MG, K=60) on Ampere, frame rate limited by
the auto-substep count in the boiling scenarios rather than by encode or
per-substep overhead. `jet` is the watch case (33.3 fps, 11 % over the a10 bar)
— any 0.6.0 change that regresses it reopens a10, so re-capture jet+cycle at
N=512/600 frames after tuning. Candidate levers, in expected payoff order:
pressure work per substep (MG cycles / K in the substep loop), the CFL safety
factor and velocity-cap policy that set ns, then per-pass cost.
