## what the spike is

The reported residual was `b - L p` with `b` and `p` from the projection and
`L` from mass and flags after transport and phase. A face weight that changes
between those two moments, times a hydrostatic pressure jump, is the whole
number. rhoVac=0.001 makes an emptied neighbor a weight of ~500.

CPU proof (`experiments/residual-spike.js`):

- drop one cell of a linear field to rhoVac: residual x499 (1.0e3 -> 5.0e5)
- melt a blocked cell inside a uniform p=5e4: residual 0 -> 2.0e5
- dam N=64, step 10: end/solve=556, solve/rhs=1.4e-4
- dam N=128, 600 steps: max drift/solve=767, drift/rhs=0.36 at step 276,
  solve/rhs stays 0.073
- cycle N=128, 1400 steps: no spike through 2.5 s, then at step 1170
  (t=4.875 s) the post-step residual is 1.2e4 times the solve and 668 times
  the solve rhs. The hardware spike is the same scenario at the same sim time
  (frame 301 is t=5.0 s, 1204 substeps). Its 26 is post-step residual over the
  post-step rhs, sampled every 30 frames at N=512, so it is a sample of this
  event rather than the same quotient. Jet at N=128 over 2.5 s does not spike.

More K makes a larger pressure, so a larger drift. That is why the spike
anti-correlates with the solve. The auto policy uses a smaller dt, moves less
mass per step, and does not spike. No solver change follows from the spike.

## changes

- GPU: on a stats frame, the last substep reduces the residual after the
  projection and before transport into stats slots 32/33. The existing stats
  pass still evaluates the post-step operator and now writes that to slots
  34/35. The two reductions do not share a bind group (`layout: "auto"`).
- CPU: `project()` stores `diag.poissonRes/poissonRhs`. `stats()` reports that
  as the solve and recomputes the post-step value as `poissonDrift`. The printed
  drift ratio uses the post-step rhs, so it is the number the older logs called
  `true`. A freeze can inflate it; health reads `true`, not `drift`.
- Report, HUD snapshot and the log table print `drift` next to `true`. The
  capture header says which is which.
- Defaults unchanged. Third level stays rejected.
