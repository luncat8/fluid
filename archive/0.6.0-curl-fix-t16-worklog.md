## review

Reviewing t6 before porting anything found a wrong derivative at the centre
of it. On this MAC layout the stored u is the west face and v the south face
of a cell, so the four faces around a cell give the divergence
(uR-uL)+(vT-vB) directly — and the t6 force's "curl", and the oracle's
"peak vorticity" since t5, was the companion combination (vT-vB)-(uR-uL).
That is dv/dy-du/dx. Three scheme-free fields say so:

- solid-body rotation (true curl 2*Omega everywhere): the old stencil reads
  exactly 0.
- pure shear u=k*y (true curl -k): exactly 0.
- the oracle's own gaussian vortex: the old stencil peaks at 4.55 on the
  diagonal (cell 43,43 at N=128) where the curl peaks in the core at 12.34
  against the analytic 2*A/sigma = 12.365.

So t6's force amplified a normal-strain difference and the oracle ranked it
by that same quantity: force and metric agreed because both were the same
wrong line of code. The eps=2/8 picks, the 3.44x/1.84x rows and the
"eps=2 is nearly free" cost row do not describe vorticity confinement, and
t5's "1.517x retained peak vorticity" is not a vorticity ratio. Everything
else in those rows was computed independently and stands: KE, divergence,
peak speed, limiter and clamp counts reproduce the 2026-10-21 logs digit
for digit after the fix, which is also the proof that the shipping path
(advectMac=0, vorticityConfinement=0) is untouched.

One smaller review catch in the same oracle: the t6 worklog says the
confinement rows are exempt from the "no new extremum" assert, but the
condition (`cfg.macLimiter || !cfg.advectMac`) never exempted them. With
the corrected force, eps=4/8 pass the initial peak speed at a checkpoint
and the assert would have crashed a sweep instead of letting the decision
block judge overdrive. Now exempt in code as documented; velClamp stays the
hard watchdog.

## what changed

- `src/core/solver.js`: the curl is dv/dx - du/dy from neighbouring
  cell-centred velocities — the average of the cell's four corner curls,
  the only centred form the staggered grid has. The free-slip convention
  moves with it: a cell-centred velocity drops a blocked face instead of
  averaging in its stored 0, a blocked or ghost neighbour mirrors the cell
  itself, and the |omega| gradient mirrors across walls the same way (it
  previously clamped only at the domain edge). Blocked cells get omega 0.
  The old openU/openV helpers duplicated uBlocked/vBlocked and are gone.
  `sim.vorticityAt(x,y)` is exposed so the oracle measures with the one
  definition instead of a copy of it.
- `experiments/vortex-decay.js`: `measure()` uses `sim.vorticityAt`;
  `curlFixedPoints` asserts the four analytic values above (solid-body
  0.2000 exact 0.2000, shear -0.3000, uniform 0, gaussian peak 12.335 vs
  analytic 12.365 at N=128); rows gained errL2 against the initial field
  (the vortex is a steady solution, so this is the row that punishes
  profile distortion — with the true curl every eps overshoots peak
  vorticity past 1, which "retained vorticity" cannot rank). Defaults
  CONF_EPS are now [1, 2].
- t16 (wgsl mac-cormack): new `advectVelMac` kernel mirroring
  `advectVelMac` in the cpu solver (same traces, clamps, stencil limiter,
  absolute clamp; the device has no macLimit/macClamp counters, like the
  forces clamp). Pass 1 reuses the shipping semi-lagrangian kernel into
  one new rg32float scratch (`pool.macTmp`) — the plan's rgba scratch with
  trace helpers is an optimization for a cost nobody has measured, so the
  port is literal first. `cfg.advectMac` now reaches the device,
  `macLimiter` is uniform slot 61, the harness takes `&mac=1`/`&maclim=0`
  and records the setting in the report header, and the capture presets
  are `mac-fixed-k20` (`python3 run_gpu_mac_fixed_k20.py`) and
  `jet-mac-auto`. Pool: 28 textures, 54.9 MB logical at N=512 (the scratch
  is 2.1 MB; allocated always, like every pool field).
- `experiments/wgsl-exec.mjs`: opt-in pre-device WGSL check on the npm
  `wgsl_reflect` CPU interpreter (outside the repo, the gpu-parity
  pattern). Parses all 29 shaders and executes the mac pair against the
  oracle. `experiments/gpu-mock.js` gained the mac cadence check (forward
  + correction dispatches between forces and rhs, per substep, only when
  the flag is on) and a writeParams check for slot 61.

| at 4 s | KE keep | peak vort keep | enstrophy keep | errL2 | maxDiv | peak speed |

| sl | 0.563 | 0.740 | 0.476 | 0.274 | 5.04e-3 | 53.7 |
| mac | 0.874 | 0.783 | 0.824 | 0.0855 | 3.20e-3 | 72.6 |
| sl+conf eps=0.5 | 0.607 | 1.279 | 0.568 | 0.233 | 5.04e-3 | 57.1 |
| sl+conf eps=1 | 0.660 | 1.796 | 0.672 | 0.201 | 5.04e-3 | 60.8 |
| sl+conf eps=2 | 0.759 | 1.962 | 0.934 | 0.161 | 5.55e-3 | 68.7 |
| sl+conf eps=4 | 0.896 | 3.458 | 1.895 | 0.173 | 1.56e-2 | 84.1 |
| sl+conf eps=8 | 1.034 | 4.318 | 4.601 | 0.259 | 7.60e-2 | 100.9 |

- t5 is re-validated on the metrics that were right and on the new one:
  mac/sl retained KE 1.553x (the plan 4.2 bar, cleared) and errL2 3.2x
  closer to the steady vortex. On the corrected vorticity the picture is
  narrower than first recorded: enstrophy keep 1.73x, peak vorticity keep
  1.058x. Mac's win is energy and profile, not core peak.
- confinement with the real curl is a different force from the one t6
  tuned. Per unit eps it is far stronger (the field it amplifies peaks
  2.7x higher and sits in the core), it now raises retained KE instead of
  lowering it, and its divergence stays at or below the shipping row up
  to eps=2. eps=1 is the conservative pick (errL2 -27 %, no divergence
  cost), eps=2 the errL2 optimum (-41 %, enstrophy keep 0.934, divergence
  1.1x) bought with a core at 1.96x its initial vorticity. eps=4/8 are
  over-driven by every row at once: errL2 turns back up, divergence
  3.1x/15x, peak speed past the initial 80. Rejected, same slot eps=32
  held in the old table.
- the ranking that matters for sequencing: mac alone (errL2 0.0855) is
  more faithful than any confinement row on the shipping scheme (best
  0.161). So t16 before t17, and eps has to be re-ranked stacked on mac
  before a wgsl port of the force is worth a dispatch — the sl rows
  above do not decide a mac build.
- cost (sandbox cpu, not the device machine): the centred curl's force
  slice is 4.0 -> ~12.4 ms/step, because centreU/centreV re-read blocked
  predicates per tap. The wgsl form is two texture passes over the
  velocity field; as with mac, neither cpu number is the device gate.
- wgsl exec (no GPU): the mac pair reproduces the cpu oracle for one step
  to 3.0e-5 on the vortex (dt=1/60 in that check), bit-exact 0 on the
  limited constant field, and 2.6e-4 on a discontinuous field where the
  cpu limiter moves 336 faces — f32 rounding scale on ±120 faces, with
  the limiter branch live. All 29 shaders parse.

## decision

Nothing new is adopted and both flags stay 0: mac-cormack's quality half
clears and its kernel now exists and is numerically checked, but the
15 % frame-time bar is a device number. Confinement is re-measured and
still has no wgsl pass; it is also demoted — port it only after the mac row
lands and after an eps sweep on top of mac says it still buys anything.

## validation

- shipping path unchanged, three ways: sl/mac KE, divergence, peak speed
  and limiter counts in the new log match `2026-10-21-vortex-decay*.log`
  digit for digit; `node experiments/residual-spike.js` reproduces its
  retained log (weight jump 1.000e+3 -> 4.995e+5, dam worst end/solve
  5.56e+2 at step 10, solve/rhs 1.356e-4); conformance, convection, phase,
  solid-growth, buoy and multigrid all pass post-change.
- `node experiments/run-all.js --quick`: 12/12 (includes gpu-mock with the
  mac cadence + slot-61 checks, gpu-memory accounting for the new texture,
  browser-tests). the remaining full-profile suites were run individually
  (the vortex row ran as the eps superset above), so all 20 full-profile
  suites have a post-change pass.
- gpu-parity could not run: this sandbox has no Vulkan loader/ICD
  (`libvulkan.so.1` absent), which is what wgsl-exec substituted for.
  A Dawn/SwiftShader or hardware parity pass over the mac path is still
  worth doing wherever a backend exists.
