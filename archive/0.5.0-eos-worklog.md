## changes

- `run_browser_tests.py` (new, project root): serves `experiments/browser-tests.html`
  on the COOP/COEP server and opens it, so `performance.now()` resolves finer than
  the clamped quantum. options: `port`, `--host`, `--no-open`, `--query` for the
  comparable-run parameters (`"n=512&frames=600&scenarios=blobs,draw&mg=0"`).
  `run_index.py` now exposes `serve(page_url, host, port, open_browser)`; both
  runners share it (no duplicated server code). README and the page help point at
  the new command.
- 0.0.0-plan.md section 5.7: added the phase-aware conduction rule the 0.5.0 plan
  demanded before phase code: harmonic-mean conductivity across phases,
  branch-interpolated capacity as a rate limiter, and the enthalpy budget
  (`0.25 * min(dist(E_L, eq(T_R)), dist(E_R, eq(T_L)))`) that keeps the four-face
  sum inside the temperature hull and stops any face from traversing a latent
  interval.
- 0.5.0-plan.md sections 3.1/3.2: corrected the output layout (therm.b/a stay the
  0.4.0 heat accumulators; sfield.rgba = (S, phi_l, phi_g, -); flags.g phase byte,
  flags.b frozen mask) and the S rule: two-sided only where phi_g > 0, one-sided
  anti-overpack for phi_g = 0, zero for the thin cold air tier — without this the
  plan-as-written collapses the scenario ambient toward rho_liq.
- src/core/solver.js: `phaseInto`/`phaseOf` (one closed form of (m,E) -> T, phi_l,
  phi_g, phase byte, rho*, S, c_eff, k), `energyFromT` (inverse curve for scenario
  and dye setup), `eqDist` (piecewise enthalpy distance). `deriveT` becomes
  `phaseEos(chargeLatent)`: writes T cache, phase byte, frozen mask, sfield, zeroes
  the four faces of a cell at its freeze flip (ledger.frozenMomentum) and charges
  m*L traversal to ledger.latentChargedE against the phi cache. poisson_rhs reads
  S one step behind. frozen faces block velocity, projection coupling and advective
  flux (shared with walls for motion) but still conduct. conduction is phase-aware
  (branch c/k, harmonic face conductivity) with the enthalpy budget; the equal-
  capacity case reduces to the old 0.5*C_h clamp exactly. stats() reports the
  phase histogram and the S-saturation watchdog.
- src/core/config.js: `conductivity` split into `kSol/kLiq/kGas` (gas nearly
  insulating keeps steam plumes hot) plus `TEps`; params slots 45-47 added,
  slot 35 now carries kLiq so the not-yet-ported WGSL keeps its 0.4.0 behavior.
- experiments/eos.js (new, t2): plateau boundaries, T(E) monotone with plateaus,
  energyFromT round trip, rho* continuity across phi_g, S signs/clamps, eqDist.
- experiments/phase.js (new, t3): melt a frozen block (frozen mask, zero flux on
  frozen faces, outside-in melting, T stays on the T_melt plateau per cell,
  latent ledger = block mass * Lf within 1%), boil a gas-branch spot (S > 0,
  expansion pushes neighbors, mass conserved, expansion factor logged vs the
  rho_liq/rho_gas target), condense a mid-boiling blob in cold liquid (phi_g -> 0,
  S < 0 contraction observed, mass conserved), freeze-flip velocity zeroing and
  frozen_momentum. Time series printed for the melt run.
- experiments/convection.js: scenario/test energies now go through energyFromT;
  `conductivity` overrides moved to `kSol/kLiq/kGas`.
- experiments/run-all.js: eos.js in quick+full, phase.js in full.
