# 0.0.0 — architecture, model and decisions

status: approved direction (from draft.txt + user answers 2026-10-18)
role: master artifact. every other plan assumes this one. read this first.
source brief: draft.txt (kept verbatim as the global task reference)

## 1. goal

a 2d webgpu fluid engine for interactive games: fast, stable, strictly conserving
mass and energy, with temperature driven convection and solid / liquid / gas states,
and color tracers that make flow and mixing visible.

## 2. non goals

- no liquid-gas free surface reconstruction, no surface tension, no droplet physics.
	interfaces are "aggregate": a cell just holds more or less substance, no special
	interface cells, no curvature, no fill-level tracking.
- no elasticity, plasticity, fracture or stress in solids. solid = immobile aggregate.
- no 3d. code is 2d only, no dimension-generic abstractions.
- no real acoustics / pressure waves / shock capturing. gas is weakly compressible
	(it can expand and push), not fully compressible.
- no legacy browsers, no webgl fallback, no build step, no modules, no dependencies.

## 3. decisions and why

| # | decision | why | rejected alternative |
|---|---|---|---|
| d1 | eulerian mac grid + variable density pressure projection | incremental (each draft milestone = a few passes), unconditionally stable velocity solve, solids are a trivial velocity mask, pressure is a first class output for the pressure view mode | lattice boltzmann — better conservation by construction, but 9-15 floats/cell, mach and low viscosity limits, harder to debug. deferred to 0.9.0 as a separate experiment |
| d2 | one substance, rgb = three conserved color tracers | user: "rgb is just color of substance ... all water and gases same". physics stays a single fluid, mixing is visible (r+g=yellow) and measurable | per channel substances with own density / melt point — more general, much more state, not needed |
| d3 | aggregate phase model, three density levels | user: "move water and gas similar way but water just not pressurized and more heavy". vacuum/air < steam < water by density, one velocity field for all, gravity sorts them out | two phase free surface solver, mpm, flip/pic |
| d4 | weakly compressible gas via a divergence source in the projection | one solver, no second gas pipeline, expansion still pushes neighbours and shows in the pressure view | fully compressible explicit solve (cfl limited by sound speed, unstable at game dt) |
| d5 | advect conserved densities with a face volume flux computed once per face per step | discrete conservation is exact by construction: the same float flux value is subtracted from one cell and added to its neighbour, so no scheme dependent leak | semi-lagrangian advection of mass/dye/energy — interpolates every step, loses and smears mass. used only for velocity |
| d6 | donor cell upwind first, then fct (zalesak) flux limiting | upwind is monotone and conservative but diffusive ("colors mix"). fct adds an anti diffusive correction as a limited flux difference, so it stays exactly conservative while removing the smear and the negative undershoot | clipping negatives to zero — destroys mass, the exact thing draft.txt forbids |
| d7 | internal energy E is the conserved thermal quantity, temperature T is derived | latent heat of melting and boiling becomes a plateau in the E to T curve, so phase change costs/releases energy for free and the energy budget closes. "keep energy and mass" is then literally true | storing T and advecting T — loses energy on mixing and cannot represent latent heat |
| d8 | boussinesq style body forces for buoyancy | one multiply add per cell, robust, no dependence on the pressure solve resolving tiny density differences | density from a full eos inside the projection alone — convection is too weak at game resolution |
| d9 | clamped pairwise exchange for conduction and viscosity | equal and opposite by construction (conservative), unconditionally stable and monotone when the clamp factor is <= 0.5, one reusable kernel for both | explicit laplacian — cfl limited by dx^2/alpha, blows up when tuning conductivity |
| d10 | fixed dt with automatic substepping | stable after tab switches, deterministic, reproducible tests and save/load | frame delta dt — explodes on hitches |
| d11 | core webgpu only: no optional features, no extensions | runs on every current webgpu browser. all our passes are ping pong (read texture A, write texture B) so write-only storage textures are enough, and we never need read_write access or float32 filtering | requesting float32-filterable / texture-formats-tier2 — narrows compatibility for no real gain |
| d12 | wgsl inline as js template strings, classic script tags | file:// blocks fetch of local files (opaque origin), and AGENTS.md forbids build and modules. inline is the only option that satisfies both | separate .wgsl files loaded by fetch |
| d13 | isomorphic cpu reference solver in src/core, wgsl in src/gpu | node can validate conservation, monotonicity, stability and budgets on small grids in milliseconds, and gpu parity tests catch shader bugs. duplication is deliberate and bounded: one scheme spec (section 5) + one conformance suite run against both | gpu only development — conservation bugs are invisible without numbers |

## 4. field model

lattice units: dx = 1, cell volume V = 1, so density numerically equals mass per cell.
grid: N x N interior cells, stored in (N+2) x (N+2) textures with a one cell ghost ring.
indices i,j run 1..N for interior. ghosts hold boundary condition values.

### 4.1 stored quantities

| quantity | symbol | meaning | conserved | notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| substance mass | m | how much stuff is in the cell | yes | m ~ 0 means vacuum/air |
| color tracers | dr, dg, db | mass of each color tracer | yes | sum is not fixed, each channel is |
| internal energy | E | sensible + latent heat | yes | T and phase are derived from it |
| velocity | u, v | face centered mac velocity | no (momentum is not a tracked invariant) | semi-lagrangian advected |
| pressure | p | projection lagrange multiplier | no | the pressure view mode reads this |
| temperature | T | derived cache of the eos | no | recomputed every step from E |
| phase fractions | phi_l, phi_g | melted / vaporized fraction in [0,1] | no | derived from E |
| flags | static, phase | walls and aggregate phase byte | no | derived, plus user painted geometry |
| divergence source | S | prescribed expansion rate | no | from phase change and gas heating |

### 4.2 derived quantities

	rho   = m                       (V = 1)
	rho_f = 2*rhoL*rhoR/(rhoL+rhoR) (harmonic mean face density, large ratio safe)
	T, phi_l, phi_g, phase = caloric_eos(E, m)   see 5.5
	rho*  = target density from phase fractions  see 5.5
	S     = (rho - rho*)/(rho * tau_phase)       see 5.4

### 4.3 density hierarchy (the aggregate model)

	rho_vac  ambient / vacuum floor, very light, keeps the projection well posed
	rho_gas  steam, light, expands when heated (rho_gas ~ 1/T)
	rho_liq  water, heavy, incompressible (rho* is constant, so S = 0)
	rho_sol  ice/rock, heavy, immobile (velocity frozen)

there are no interface cells. a cell between water and air simply has an
intermediate m, which makes the interface blobby and gives the emergent
splashing the user asked for without any surface model.

## 5. scheme specification (single source of truth)

this section is the contract shared by src/core (cpu reference) and src/gpu (wgsl).
if the two disagree, this section wins and both get fixed.

### 5.1 face flux

	fx(i,j) = u(i,j) * dt   volume flux through the left face of cell (i,j), +x direction
	fy(i,j) = v(i,j) * dt   volume flux through the bottom face of cell (i,j), +y direction
	fx,fy = 0 on any face where either neighbour is static solid or frozen solid
	fx,fy = 0 on the domain boundary faces (closed box) or set by the open boundary mode

computed once per step by pass compute_flux into texture flux (rg32float).
both cells sharing a face read that one value: cell (i,j) reads fx(i,j) and
fx(i+1,j), cell (i+1,j) reads fx(i+1,j). identical float, opposite sign, so the
global sum cannot drift.

### 5.2 conservative advection of a density q (q = m, dr, dg, db, E)

	low order (donor cell upwind):
	q'(i,j) = q(i,j) + fx(i,j)*qup(i-1,j -> i,j) - fx(i+1,j)*qup(i,j -> i+1,j)
	               + fy(i,j)*qup(i,j-1 -> i,j) - fy(i,j+1)*qup(i,j -> i,j+1)
	qup(a -> b) = q(a) if the flux enters b from a, else q(b)   (sign of the flux decides)

	high order correction (fct / zalesak, added in 0.3.1):
	dq(f) = high order flux(f) - low order flux(f)      per face, antisymmetric
	limited by the bounds of the low order solution so no new extremum appears
	and no density goes negative, then applied as a face difference like above

	cfl requirement: |u|*dt <= 1 per axis for monotonicity. enforced by substepping.

all conserved densities ride the same flux field in the same pass, so a single
kernel advects m, dr, dg, db, E together. ratios (color per unit mass) stay
bounded by the upwind values, which is what keeps r+g looking yellow instead of
turning into a uniform brown.

### 5.3 velocity advection (semi-lagrangian)

	for each face: backtrace x0 = x_face - dt * u_interpolated(x_face)
	sample the velocity field bilinearly at x0, write to the ping pong target
	mac interpolation: sample u from the u face lattice, v from the v face lattice
unconditionally stable at any cfl, mildly diffusive. acceptable because momentum
is not a tracked invariant. upgraded to mac-cormack + limiter in 0.6.0 if needed.

### 5.4 projection (variable density, with expansion source)

	solve   div( (1/rho_f) * grad p ) = div(u*)/dt - S
	apply   u = u* - dt * (p_right - p_left) / rho_f   per face

	jacobi update for interior cell c:
	w_f   = 1/rho_f for each of the 4 faces that are not blocked
	p_c   = ( sum_f w_f * p_nb(f) - b_c ) / sum_f w_f
	b_c   = ( u(i+1,j) - u(i,j) + v(i,j+1) - v(i,j) ) / dt - S(i,j)

	blocked face (static solid, frozen solid, wall): dropped from both sums.
	open boundary mode: p = 0 dirichlet on the selected edge instead of dropping.
	free slip by default on domain edges (normal velocity zero, tangential free),
	no slip as a tunable for walls.

S comes from the previous step's phase pass (operator split, first order in dt,
intentional). S is clamped to |S| <= S_max to keep the solve well behaved.

### 5.5 caloric eos and phase

	constants: c_sol, c_liq, c_gas, L_f (fusion), L_v (vaporization),
	           T_melt, T_boil, rho_liq, rho_gas0
	datum: E = 0 for solid at T = 0

	E1 = m * c_sol * T_melt
	E2 = E1 + m * L_f
	E3 = E2 + m * c_liq * (T_boil - T_melt)
	E4 = E3 + m * L_v

	E < E1        solid     T = E/(m*c_sol)                      phi_l = 0, phi_g = 0
	E1 <= E < E2  melting   T = T_melt                           phi_l = (E-E1)/(m*L_f)
	E2 <= E < E3  liquid    T = T_melt + (E-E2)/(m*c_liq)        phi_l = 1
	E3 <= E < E4  boiling   T = T_boil                           phi_g = (E-E3)/(m*L_v)
	E >= E4       gas       T = T_boil + (E-E4)/(m*c_gas)        phi_g = 1

	phase byte = caloric branch: 1 solid, 2 melting, 3 liquid, 4 boiling, 5 gas
	             vacuum if m < m_eps (phase 0, no eos evaluation)

	target density (mass weighted specific volume, this is the "mix properly" rule):
	v*    = (1 - phi_g)/rho_liq + phi_g/rho_gas(T)
	rho_gas(T) = rho_gas0 * T_boil / max(T, T_eps)
	rho*  = 1/v*    (solid and liquid share rho_liq: solids do not expand)

	expansion source:
	S = clamp( (rho - rho*) / (rho * tau_phase), -S_max, S_max )

freezing: as E falls through [E1, E2], phi_l decreases at T_melt. transport
removes energy; never add m*L_f to E on a phase change (that double counts latent
heat). block faces when phi_l < 0.5 and phi_g == 0; recompute this same mask before
the next projection and use it consistently in projection, velocity and flux passes.
The phase byte describes the caloric branch (0 vacuum, 1 solid, 2 melting,
3 liquid, 4 boiling, 5 gas); the face-blocking predicate is separate.

aggregate crystal growth is a thermal front, not a mass source: cooling through an
existing solid lets neighbouring liquid lose latent energy and freeze in place.
No snapping m to rho_sol, no dye expulsion, no extra latent source. Solid-fluid faces
block advection but still conduct heat. Baseline equilibrium freezing allows new
solid anywhere sufficiently cold; seeded-only growth / supercooling would require
additional kinetic state and is deferred (0.5.0 section 3.8).

### 5.6 buoyancy and gravity

	a_mass = g_vec * (rho_ref - rho) / rho_ref      heavy cells sink, light cells rise
	a_heat = -g_vec * beta * (T - T_ref)            hot rises
	vel += dt * (a_mass + a_heat)
the projection removes any spatially uniform part, so only differences matter.

### 5.7 conduction and viscosity (clamped pairwise exchange)

	for each face between cells L and R, for a scalar q with capacity C:
	exchange = coef * (q_R - q_L) * dt
	C_h = (C_L * C_R)/(C_L + C_R)
	limit exchange to 0.5 * C_h * (q_R - q_L)     <- stable and monotone for any coef
	q_L += exchange / C_L ; q_R -= exchange / C_R

	conduction: q = T, C = m * c(phase), coef = conductivity
	viscosity:  q = tangential velocity, C = rho, coef = viscosity
equal and opposite by construction, so total energy and momentum are untouched.
the clamp means "very conductive" degrades to "fast but rate limited", never to
an explosion.

### 5.8 emitters and sinks (accounted)

every injection or removal goes through the source ledger:
	injected_m, injected_dr/dg/db, injected_E, removed_*, work_done_by_forces
so the expected total is computable and drift = actual - expected is meaningful.
mouse stirring adds momentum only (not mass), so it is work, not a mass source.

## 6. conservation contract

invariants (closed box, no emitters):

	sum(m), sum(dr), sum(dg), sum(db), sum(E)      exactly constant to float rounding
	sum(momentum)                                  constant only with gravity off

ledger form (emitters on):

	actual_total == initial_total + injected - removed - boundary_losses

measurement:

	hierarchical reduction pass -> stats buffer -> async readback -> hud
	per invariant, the hud shows: total, expected, drift_abs, drift_rel
	cpu reference reports the same numbers in experiments/logs

error budget (acceptance, measured in 0.3.0):

	dye and mass relative drift < 1e-6 after 1000 steps at 256x256
	min(m), min(dr/dg/db) >= 0 at all times (monotonicity, not clipping)
	energy budget closure (injected == stored + lost) < 0.1 % over 1000 steps
	no f16 storage for any conserved quantity, ever. rgba32float only.

## 7. gpu memory layout

textures, all (N+2)x(N+2), all ping pong where a stencil reads and writes:

| name | format | bytes/cell | copies | content |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| vel | rg32float | 8 | 2 | u at left face, v at bottom face |
| flux | rg32float | 8 | 1 | fx, fy volume flux, same layout |
| mass | rgba32float | 16 | 2 | r=dr, g=dg, b=db, a=m |
| therm | rgba32float | 16 | 2 | r=E, g=T, b=phi_l, a=phi_g |
| press | r32float | 4 | 2 | pressure |
| divsrc | r32float | 4 | 1 | rhs of the poisson solve |
| sfield | r32float | 4 | 1 | expansion source S |
| limiter | rg32float | 8 | 1 | fct R_plus, R_minus per cell (0.3.1) |
| flags | rgba8uint | 4 | 2 | r=static mask, g=phase, b=debug, a=spare |
| display | rgba16float | 8 | 1 | colormapped output for the render pass |

buffers: params (uniform, f32 array, fixed offsets), stats (storage, f32),
stats_part (storage, f32, one slot per workgroup), obstacles upload staging.

budget at N=512 (514^2 = 264196 cells): about 34 MB total. at N=1024: about 135 MB.
default N=256 during development, 512 as the performance target (see 0.6.0).

display is rgba16float because that format is filterable in core webgpu, so the
render pass can bilinear upscale the grid without requesting float32-filterable.
sim fields are read with textureLoad (integer coords), never with a sampler.

| # | pass | in | out | notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | forces | vel, therm, mass, params | vel | gravity, boussinesq, mouse, emitters |
| 2 | advect_vel | vel | vel' | semi-lagrangian |
| 3 | poisson_rhs | vel', sfield, mass | divsrc | div(u*)/dt - S |
| 4 | project_iter xK | divsrc, press, mass, flags | press' | jacobi, ping pong, K tunable |
| 5 | apply_grad | press', vel', mass | vel | u -= dt*grad(p)/rho_f |
| 6 | freeze_solid | vel, flags | vel | zero faces touching frozen/static solid |
| 7 | boundary | all | all | ghost ring, walls, open edges |
| 8 | compute_flux | vel, flags, params | flux | one value per face |
| 9 | advect_scalar | flux, mass, therm | mass', therm' | section 5.2 low order |
| 10 | limit_flux | flux, mass', therm' | mass'', therm'' | fct correction (0.3.1) |
| 11 | derive_T | therm'', mass'' | therm'' | T cached in therm.g (folded into phase_eos from 0.5.0) |
| 12 | conduction | therm'', mass'' | therm | section 5.7 clamped pairwise exchange, ping pong |
| 13 | heat_sources | therm, params, flags | therm | heaters, boundary cooling, viscous work |
| 14 | phase_eos | therm, mass | therm', flags', sfield | section 5.5: T, phi_l, phi_g, phase byte, S |
| 15 | stats (every Fs frames) | mass, therm, vel, press | stats_part, stats | two level reduction |
| 16 | to_display | mass, therm, press, vel, flags, params | display | colormap per view mode |
| 17 | render pass | display | canvas | fullscreen triangle, bilinear sample |

one command encoder, one compute pass, one render pass, one submit per frame.
webgpu orders dispatches inside a compute pass and inserts the barriers, so no
manual synchronization is needed between passes, only ping pong for stencils.

module convention: each file is an iife that attaches to the global Fluid
namespace (Fluid.core, Fluid.gpu, Fluid.app) and ends with

	if (typeof module !== "undefined" && module.exports) module.exports = Fluid.core.eos;

so node can require src/core/* directly while the browser uses plain script tags.
src/gpu/* may also export but never runs without a device.

## 10. runtime constraints

- file:// must work: no fetch of local files (opaque origin), no modules, no build,
	no cdn. all wgsl inline, all scripts local, relative src paths.
- webgpu needs a secure context. file:// is treated as trustworthy by chrome and
	firefox, but this is browser dependent and is verified first thing in 0.1.0.
	runtime probe prints a clear on screen message plus the local server fallback
	(python3 -m http.server from the repo root) instead of failing silently.
- core webgpu defaults only: maxComputeInvocationsPerWorkgroup 256 (128 in
	compatibility mode) so workgroup size is 8x8 = 64 by default, maxComputeWorkgroups
	PerDimension 65535, maxComputeWorkgroupStorageSize 16 KB, maxUniformBufferBinding
	Size 64 KB. no requiredFeatures, no requiredLimits beyond defaults.
- storage textures: core formats only, and read_write access only exists for
	r32uint/r32sint/r32float. we never use read_write; every pass is ping pong with a
	read (or textureLoad) source and a write-only destination.
- no float atomics in core wgsl, so all reductions are hierarchical
	(workgroup shared memory -> partial buffer -> final buffer). this also keeps
	results order independent, hence deterministic and safe for save/load and tests.
- float32 textures are not filterable without the float32-filterable feature:
	always textureLoad them. filtered output goes through the rgba16float display texture.
- readback never blocks the frame: copy to a staging buffer, mapAsync once, keep a
	"readback in flight" flag, consume the result in a later frame.
- no allocations in the frame loop: bind groups, pipelines, textures, typed arrays,
	uniform staging and the command encoder pattern are all created at setup or on
	resize. the hud text update is the single acknowledged exception, throttled to 5 hz.

## 13. validation strategy

- every plan ends with measurable acceptance criteria, not "looks ok".
- src/core is the numeric oracle: invariants, monotonicity, budgets, cfl sweeps run
	in node in under a second on a 64x64 grid, logs kept in experiments/logs.
- gpu parity: identical initial state and dt on cpu and gpu, readback and compare
	max/mean absolute error per field. catches layout, sign, ghost and unit bugs.
- in browser: hud conservation meters are always visible, plus debug view modes
	(divergence residual, negative density, pressure, velocity, phase overlay).
- a scenario is only "done" when the numbers in experiments/logs and the on screen
	meters both agree with the acceptance criteria of its plan.

## 14. open questions

- oq1 does the liquid need a mild expansion source too, or is boussinesq enough for
	convincing convection? decided by measurement in 0.4.0, toggle provided.
- oq2 open top boundary (steam escapes, mass leaves the ledger) or always closed box?
	default closed, open edge implemented in 0.2.0 and accounted in the ledger.
- oq3 is jacobi with K=40 enough at 512^2 for a large density ratio, or does the
	projection need multigrid from the start? measured in 0.2.0, multigrid in 0.6.0.
- oq4 does semi-lagrangian velocity advection smear vortices enough to matter for
	gameplay? if yes, mac-cormack in 0.6.0.
- oq5 how much solid mechanics is enough: pure freeze, or freeze plus slow creep
	under gravity (angle of repose)? default pure freeze, revisit after 0.5.0.
