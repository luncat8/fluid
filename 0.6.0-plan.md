# 0.6.0 — performance and quality

status: in progress. t1 (guarded timestamp-query profiling in HUD + browser
report) done; t4 was completed as part of 0.5.0 (two-level multigrid is the
default solver, red-black remains as mg=0). The Ampere N=512 auto-policy
baseline is recorded; the fixed-policy A/B baseline, N=1024, and per-group GPU
times on hardware are the open measurements.
depends on: 0.5.0 (a complete sim to optimize; optimizing earlier measures the wrong thing)
milestone: draft.txt "fast, stable, good for interactive games"

## 1. goal

hit 60 fps at N=512 with the full pass graph on a mid range gpu, keep 30 fps at N=1024
as a stretch, and improve visual quality where the current schemes are visibly weak
(soft vortices, over diffused dye, slow projection convergence). every change is measured
against a recorded baseline, and nothing is adopted on intuition.

## 2. scope

in: per pass gpu timing, multigrid projection (red-black is the baseline), mac-cormack velocity
advection, vorticity confinement, half float for non conserved fields, pass fusion,
workgroup tuning, automatic quality scaling, benchmark scripts and logs.

out: any change to the conserved quantity path (m, dye, E stay rgba32float and stay
flux advected — that is the contract of 0.0.0 section 6), 3d, api work (0.7.0).

## 3. baseline and measurement

recorded baseline (experiments/logs/2026-10-20-04_36-GPU-browser-tests.log,
sign-off in archive/0.5.0-hardware-signoff-worklog.md): N=512, full graph, K=60,
FCT on, MG 2 cycles pre4/post8, auto substeps, Nvidia Ampere: ice 133.5, rain
67.9, arch 46.8, cycle 43.3, jet 33.3 fps over 600 frames each, ~2.1-2.6 ms per
substep, CPU encode <= 0.29 ms, drift_rel <= 3.3e-6. still open before adopting
any optimization: the fixed-policy baseline below at N=512 and N=1024, per-group
GPU ms (measurable since t1 via &profile=1), memory, and the ns histogram. The
user's earlier N=1024 report (~11 fps, K=60, up to 8 substeps) is diagnostic
context, not a controlled benchmark.

pin the step policy in every row. frame cost scales with the substep count, so a
number without `substepsAuto` / `substepsFixed` / observed `ns` is not comparable.
run two baselines: the policy off at 4 fixed steps (a fixed dispatch count, for A/B
comparisons of solver work) and the policy on (the user-facing number). when the
policy is on, also record the ns histogram over the 300 measured frames.

account for readbacks. stats copy 128 B every 30 frames and the buoy sample copies
24 B every 4 frames; both use `mapAsync`, and a sample is skipped while a readback is
already in flight. record mapAsync latency, skipped samples and whether the queue
stalls. if either shows up in the frame trace, the candidate fixes are a 2-3 entry
ring of MAP_READ buffers or one combined copy for stats + buoy, not a bigger cadence.

time the display separately from the sim. `to_display` is one compute pass plus a
fullscreen render every frame; at N=1024 it moves 4x the pixels of N=512 and can
dominate on integrated gpus. the paused path already redraws only when dirty, so the
measurement is the running case.

timing: implemented (t1). the device requests timestamp-query only when the adapter
offers it; src/gpu/profiler.js times five groups per substep (vel, rhs, press,
trans, phase) plus stats/disp/render per frame through a 2-slot readback ring.
profiled frames split the single compute pass at group boundaries (identical
dispatch sequence), so read group proportions, not absolutes; the fallback when
the feature is absent is the existing queue-wait number. per-group ms go into
the hud debug block and into experiments/logs.

benchmarks:
	experiments/browser-tests.html  in browser: per-scenario captures; solver sweeps
	                             via query params (&k= &cycles= &pre= &post= &s=
	                             &auto= &ns= &mg=0), per-group GPU ms via &profile=1;
	                             the report is copy-pasteable into logs
	experiments/mg-work*.js      node: solver-work studies on the reference solver
	                             (residual vs work per config), which is where scheme
	                             variants get tried first because it is 100x faster
	                             to iterate

## 4. candidates (each with a decision rule)

### 4.1 projection solver

	baseline: the shipping projection is the two-level multigrid V-cycle (defect
	correction, coarsen S=4, open-count Neumann across blocked cells): 2 cycles,
	pre4/post8 fine red-black sweeps, K=60 coarse sweeps per cycle, pressure
	warm-started across substeps. plain red-black (K sweeps, two dispatches per
	sweep) is the mg=0 fallback. plain jacobi stays rejected: the checkerboard
	mode is an exact eigenvalue -1 on the closed box, so it does not converge at any K.
	variants now: cycle count (1 vs 2), smoother counts (pre/post), coarse K;
	            later: 3+ levels, damped-jacobi smoother
	decision: adopt the variant that reaches residual 1e-3 at N=512 in the least gpu time.
	the residual that counts is the true Poisson residual (residualNorm/divBefore
	on the oracle): divAfter floors at max|S| in boiling scenarios and hides
	solver quality entirely (measured, mg-work logs).
	measured on the CPU oracle at N=128 (experiments/logs/2026-10-20-mg-work*.log):
	the second V-cycle buys ~20x lower divergence on dam (relRes 4.6e-4 vs 9.2e-3
	at 1 cycle) — it is not waste; coarse K is the cheap knob (60 -> 20 unchanged
	at 1 cycle, fewer tiny dispatches); pre2/post4 is REJECTED: ~2.4x worse
	residual and unstable (NaN blowup in cycle) — under-smoothed restriction at
	coefficient jumps injects energy. hardware A/B of the surviving candidates
	(K cut at 2 cycles; 1 vs 2 cycles) with &profile=1 closes this item.
	note: multigrid on a variable coefficient poisson with a 1000x density ratio can stall;
	the residual metric from 0.2.0 is the judge. if it stalls, keep the red-black baseline
	and raise K, not jacobi.

### 4.2 velocity advection

	variants: semi-lagrangian (current), mac-cormack (two semi-lagrangian passes with a
	correction), mac-cormack + min-mod limiter, plus optional vorticity confinement force.
	decision: adopt if the stirred vortex decay improves by >= 25 % for <= 15 % extra frame
	time. mac-cormack can produce overshoot, so the limiter is mandatory and the min/max
	watchdog must stay at 0.
	vorticity confinement is a force, not an advection change: cheap, big visual payoff for
	games, default off until measured.

### 4.3 formats and bandwidth

	conserved (mass, therm): stay rgba32float. non negotiable.
	candidates for rgba16float/rg16float: vel, press, divsrc, sfield, flux, limiter.
	decision: adopt per field only if drift_rel and the residual are unchanged at the
	baseline tolerance and frame time improves. flux is the risky one: a half precision
	flux still conserves exactly (both cells read the same stored half value) but changes
	the transported amount, so it is allowed; press affects only the solve quality.
	pass fusion: derive_T into conduction, heat_sources into phase_eos, freeze_solid into
	compute_flux. each fusion saves a full field read+write. decision: adopt when it saves
	more than 3 % frame time and does not make the kernel harder to read (AGENTS.md: flat,
	no deep nesting).
	ping pong reduction: fields with a single writer and no stencil (sfield, divsrc) do not
	need two copies.

### 4.4 dispatch and occupancy

	workgroup size sweep: 8x8, 16x8, 16x16, 32x8 (16x16 = 256 is the core limit, 8x8 = 64
	is the compatibility mode safe choice). stencil kernels often prefer 16x8 or 8x8 because
	of the shared texture cache line layout.
	early return cost: kernels that discard out of range invocations still occupy slots;
	for ghost padded grids, dispatch exactly the interior size and offset the index instead.
	one compute pass vs several: measure whether splitting helps (it usually does not, but
	the driver barrier behaviour varies).

### 4.5 automatic quality

	a controller that reads the smoothed frame ms and adjusts, in this order:
		projection iteration count, fct on/off, grid N (with a full realloc, hysteresis
		of at least 2 s), substep cap
	target band 12-16 ms. off by default (developers want fixed settings), on for the
	integration build (0.7.0). must never touch dt in a way that changes conservation, and
	must log every change so a quality drop is explainable.

### 4.6 readback and display

	readbacks: the stats reduction and the buoy sample both end in `mapAsync`. a skipped
	sample is not a correctness bug (the body holds its last force), but it becomes a
	behaviour change under load. variants: current single-slot, a 2-3 entry MAP_READ ring,
	or one copy that carries stats + buoy. decision: adopt the one that
	keeps the readback out of the frame trace at 60 fps with the smallest extra memory.
	when 0.7.0 adds the probe table, its readback shares this path rather than becoming a
	third copy. any ring needs the same generation guard that stats and buoy already use,
	so a readback that lands after reset or resize is dropped.
	display: variants are the current N-sized rgba16float display with a fullscreen
	render, a display texture sized to the css pixels (filtered upscale from N), and a
	reduced dispatch that writes every other cell. decision: adopt only if the pooled
	image SSIM/diff against the current view is acceptable and the pass timing improves;
	the numbers on screen must not change because of a view optimization.
	frame pacing: report p50, p95 and 1 % low separately. a 60 fps average with a 30 fps
	1 % low reads as a stutter, not a success.

## 6. acceptance

- a1 60 fps (frame ms <= 16.6) at N=512 with the full graph and the default quality on the
	dev machine, and a documented number for a mid range integrated gpu if one is available.
- a2 divergence residual below 1e-3 at N=512 within a 3 ms projection budget.
- a3 conservation unchanged: a2/a3 of 0.3.0 still pass exactly after every optimization.
- a4 dye front width not worse than the 0.3.1 number, vortex decay improved by >= 25 % if
	mac-cormack is adopted.
- a5 gpu memory below 60 MB at N=512, below 200 MB at N=1024.
- a6 every adopted change has a before/after row in experiments/logs; every rejected
	candidate has a row with the reason. no undocumented "it felt faster".
- a7 automatic quality keeps the frame time inside the target band through a scripted
	load change (grid painted full of obstacles, then cleared) without oscillation.

## 7. risks and pitfalls

- optimizing before the baseline exists: everything in this plan is invalid without t2.
- half precision creep into the conserved path "just for one field" — the ledger will not
	catch it immediately, so the rule is written in 0.0.0 section 6 and repeated here.
- multigrid with a variable coefficient and huge density ratios can converge worse than
	red-black on the coarse levels; use a coefficient aware restriction (harmonic mean) and
	verify on the dam break scenario, not on a uniform box.
- mac-cormack overshoot without a limiter looks like sparkling noise at interfaces.
- pass fusion tends to produce deeply nested kernels, which AGENTS.md forbids; if a fused
	kernel needs more than two nesting levels, do not fuse it.
- timestamp-query is an optional feature: never make the sim depend on it.
- frame time measurements without a warmed up pipeline and without vsync accounted for are
	noise. measure over 300 frames, discard the first 60, report median, p95 and 1 % low.
- an unpinned substep policy silently rewrites the baseline: the same solver at 4 and 8
	substeps differs by ~2x. every before/after pair names the policy and the observed ns.
- readback stalls look like sim cost in a submit-time measurement. keep GPU timestamp
	numbers and wall-clock submit numbers in separate columns.
