## 1. goal

hit 60 fps at N=512 with the full pass graph on a mid range gpu, keep 30 fps at N=1024
as a stretch, and improve visual quality where the current schemes are visibly weak
(soft vortices, over diffused dye, slow projection convergence). every change is measured
against a recorded baseline, and nothing is adopted on intuition.

## 2. scope

in: per pass gpu timing, multigrid projection (red-black is the baseline), mac-cormack velocity
advection, vorticity confinement, half float for non conserved fields, pass fusion,
workgroup tuning, automatic quality scaling, benchmark scripts and logs.

out: any change to the conserved quantity path (m, dye, E stay rgba32float and stay
flux advected — that is the contract of 0.0.0 section 6), 3d, api work (0.7.0).

## 4. candidates (each with a decision rule)

### 4.1 projection solver

	baseline: the shipping projection is the two-level multigrid V-cycle (defect
	correction, coarsen S=4, open-count Neumann across blocked cells): 2 cycles,
	pre4/post8 fine red-black sweeps, K=20 coarse sweeps per cycle, pressure
	warm-started across substeps. plain red-black (K sweeps, two dispatches per
	sweep) is the mg=0 fallback. plain jacobi stays rejected: the checkerboard
	mode is an exact eigenvalue -1 on the closed box, so it does not converge at any K.
	variants now: cycle count (1 vs 2), smoother counts (pre/post), coarse K;
	            later: 3+ levels, damped-jacobi smoother
	decision: adopt the variant that reaches residual 1e-3 at N=512 in the least gpu time.
	the residual that counts is the true Poisson residual of the system just solved
	(diag.poissonRes/divBefore, captured at the end of project, before transport):
	divAfter floors at max|S| in boiling scenarios and hides solver quality entirely
	(measured, mg-work logs). calling residualNorm() after step() is not that number:
	transport and phase change the face weights, and one changed face next to a
	hydrostatic jump reads as a huge residual (dam N=64, end/solve=556 at a step whose
	solve/rhs is 1.4e-4). the hardware report prints both: slots 32/33 are the solve
	(`poisson: res= rhs= true=`), slots 34/35 are the post-step reading (`drift=`).
	the 2026-10-21 fixed-dt cycle spike (true=26 at frame 301) is the drift column.
	the CPU cycle at N=128 hits the same event (step 1170, t=4.9 s, post-step residual
	1.2e4 times the solve). confirmed on the same Ampere box, same preset, after the
	split (`2026-10-21-07_19`): drift=26.11078936008509 at frame 301,
	driftRes=49994.73828125, identical to the pre-split absRes; end-of-run drift
	equals the old true (0.009715138446710316) while the solve reads 0.00877. the
	sampled `true` peak is 0.141 at frame 1. mass, phases, pressure range and maxDiv
	match the pre-split capture to the printed digits, so the trajectory did not move.
	it anti-correlates with solver work, so it must not rank a candidate. cost of the
	solve capture: two dispatches on the sampled frame's last substep, inside the
	press timestamp group, before transport. the 07_19 press sample is 0.03 ms above
	the pre-split row; that is the extra reduce, not a solver change.
	measured on the CPU oracle (experiments/logs/2026-10-20-mg-samestate*.log,
	mg-work*.log; pinned dt=1/240; the controlled rows run one step from a shared
	warm state because a multi-step peak metric follows the trajectory):
	- coarse K, same state: 60 -> 20 costs +16 % peak divergence on dam and blobs
	  at N=128 (+8 % at N=512) with the true Poisson residual unchanged
	  (+0.01 % / +0.07 %), and nothing in jet/cycle. It removes 160 of the 295
	  dispatches per projection. 60 -> 40 costs +4-6 % and removes 80; see the
	  a2 note below.
	- the second V-cycle is worth ~21x lower peak divergence on dam and blobs
	  (same state, N=128: relRes 3.7e-4 vs 7.7e-3; true residual +4.6 % / +10.8 %),
	  but is free in jet/cycle.
	- pre2/post4 is REJECTED: ~2.4x worse residual and unstable (NaN blowup in
	  cycle) — under-smoothed restriction at coefficient jumps injects energy.
	adopted: 2 cycles, pre4/post8, coarse K=20 (cfg.mgCoarseIters; the mg=0
	fallback keeps its own 60 red-black sweeps in cfg.pressureIters).
	the fixed-policy hardware rows price the ladder (N=512, ns=4, dt=1/240,
	blobs/dam/cycle/jet): K60 72.6/72.5/73.6/71.8 fps, K40 86.1/86.3/87.9/86.2,
	K20 103.8/103.9/104.9/103.3, K20-at-1-cycle 161.3/159.2/160.4/161.5. press fits
	`press = 0.026 + cycles*(0.466 + 0.0071*K)` ms/substep (predicts K40 within 1.2 %),
	so a coarse sweep costs 7.1 us and everything that is not the coarse solve —
	the 12 fine sweeps and the transfers — costs 466 us per cycle. coarse K is
	therefore nearly exhausted: 60 -> 20 buys 31 % of press and 20 -> 0 would buy
	only 23 %, while one cycle removed buys 49 % of press. that is why the cycle
	count, not a smaller K, is the next lever, and why a third level (which shrinks
	both terms) beats a fourth tuning of K.
	1 cycle is NOT the default. at N=128 it leaves relRes 7.7e-3 (dam) and 4.3e-3
	(blobs), 21-22x the 2-cycle row and above the 1e-3 bar; the fixed-policy hardware
	row agrees (blobs sampled maxDiv 2.81 -> 6.27, dam ends the 600 frames with 1191
	overpacked cells where every 2-cycle row ends with 0). the old "residual-neutral
	in jet/cycle" claim was the post-step column, and a same-state step inherits a
	2-cycle pressure. re-measured on the solve residual
	(`experiments/logs/2026-10-21-mg-cycle-gate.log`): dam/blobs solve is 18-19x worse
	while drift moves only 5-11 %; cycle is 1.00x on one inherited step and 27x on a
	trajectory that has been 1-cycle the whole time (peakDivA 37x), which matches the
	hardware maxDiv 30 -> 122. jet is the only neutral row (solve 1.03x same-state,
	1.00x over its own 180 steps).
	adopted for jet only, after the fixed-ns pair on the new true column
	(`2026-10-21-08_02` and `...1`, N=512, ns=4, K20, Ampere):
	- settled true 0.01145 -> 0.01000 (not worse), drift 0.0123 -> 0.0106
	- sampled true peak is the frame-1 cold start, 0.067 -> 0.124. it does not
	  persist; both rows' drift equals true there, so it is a first solve, not
	  coefficient drift. not a ranking.
	- maxDiv 27.87 -> 23.70, end maxDiv 22.29 -> 21.18, maxVel 109.62 -> 109.54,
	  over=0, conservation unchanged. pressure span 4458 -> 4369; the mean shift
	  is the Neumann null space, not a different gradient.
	- fps 104.8 -> 157.6, press 1.219 -> 0.641 ms/substep.
	the product gate lives in prepareScenario and is off unless the app or the
	browser harness sets productCycleGate, so oracle scripts keep the knob they
	named. `&cycles=` pins the count. the auto-policy row is measured and clears
	60 fps (76.3 at ns=12, press 0.609 ms, settled true 0.00362, maxVel 260, end
	maxDiv 21.0, `experiments/logs/2026-10-21-20_46-GPU-browser-tests.log`; full
	4-scenario auto baseline re-verified at 71.8 / 67.9 / 71.6 / 76.8 fps in
	`experiments/logs/2026-10-23-04_54-GPU-browser-tests.log`);
	`python3 run_gpu_capture.py jet-auto` reproduces it. do not extend
	the gate to cycle, dam or blobs.
	a2 note: on multi-step rows the K=20 default reads rel 3.1e-4 (N=64), 7.7e-4
	(N=128) and 1.9e-3 (N=256); K=40 reads 2.5e-4, 4.6e-4 and 8.8e-4, so it is the
	fallback that holds the 1e-3 bar at every measured resolution. K=40 now has its
	hardware row too: taking it costs 0.28 ms/substep, i.e. 17 % of the fps at
	N=512 (104 -> 87) and 17-19 % in the auto policy. K=20 stays the default because
	its controlled true residual is unchanged (-0.04 % at N=512) and a2 is stated as
	a settled-state target that even the K=60 reference misses during the N=512
	splash (5.5e-3); `&k=40` reverses the trade without a rebuild, and the residual
	channel in the hardware report is the measurement that can settle it at N=512.
	note: multigrid on a variable coefficient poisson with a 1000x density ratio can stall;
	the residual metric from 0.2.0 is the judge. if it stalls, keep the red-black baseline
	and raise K, not jacobi.

### 4.2 velocity advection

	variants: semi-lagrangian (current), mac-cormack (two semi-lagrangian passes with a
	correction), mac-cormack + min-mod limiter, plus optional vorticity confinement force.
	decision: adopt if the stirred vortex decay improves by >= 25 % for <= 15 % extra frame
	time. mac-cormack can produce overshoot, so the limiter is mandatory and the min/max
	watchdog must stay at 0.
	measured (cpu oracle `experiments/vortex-decay.js`, `python3 run_vortex_decay.py`;
	log `2026-10-22-vortex-decay-conf.log`, which carries the advection and the
	confinement rows; the 2026-10-21 logs' KE, divergence, peak-speed and clamp
	rows are unchanged to the printed digits, but their vorticity/enstrophy rows
	measured dv/dy-du/dx in place of the curl and are superseded): a gaussian vortex, rim
	80 cells/s, sigma=N/6, N=128, dt=1/240 (0.33 cells per step, the product's), gravity
	and heat off, viscosity 0, so every joule lost is numerical diffusion. the loop is
	velocity only and the projection is the shipping one (mg 2 cycles, K20). errL2 is
	the distance from the initial field (the vortex is a steady solution), the row
	that punishes profile distortion the keep ratios cannot see.
	- retained kinetic energy: at 1 s sl 0.838, mac 0.963 -> 1.149x. at 4 s sl 0.563,
	  mac 0.874 -> 1.553x. errL2 at 4 s: sl 0.274, mac 0.0855. enstrophy keep
	  0.476 -> 0.824; peak vorticity keep is nearly a tie (0.740 -> 0.783, 1.058x):
	  mac's win is energy and profile, not core peak. maxDiv at 4 s 5.0e-3 -> 3.2e-3.
	- the 1 s window cannot clear the bar at all: no scheme can beat 1/keep, and sl has
	  only lost 16 % by then, so the ceiling is 1.193x. the ratio is a function of the
	  vortex age; the bar has to name the window. 4 s is where sl has decayed enough to
	  separate the schemes, and it is the row the decision reads.
	- the limiter is mandatory and the watchdog is the proof. without it and without a
	  projection the scheme creates energy: peak speed 80 -> 105 -> 270 -> 400 -> 441
	  over 4 s, retained KE 1.31, and vClamp trips 256 times. with
	  it, peak speed never leaves 80.00 and vClamp stays 0. the mechanism is the wall:
	  a backward trace from the face next to a blocked face reads that face's 0, so the
	  raw correction extrapolates past the boundary value by 7.7 % per step, compounding
	  (constant-field test, `--no-proj` row). the projection cleans it up per step, which
	  is why the limited and raw rows agree to 6 digits when it is on.
	- cost: the cpu step goes 52.4 -> 63.9 ms (+22 %, wall clock, a few percent of run
	  variance), but the advection slice is only 4.46 -> 12.38 ms (+178 %) and is 9 % of
	  that step; the rest is dominated by the projection.
	  on the device the vel group is 0.033 ms of a 1.448 ms substep sum (2 %,
	  `2026-10-21-05_55` jet), so tripling advection is not tripling the frame.
	- device row at pinned ns=4 (`experiments/logs/2026-10-23-04_53-GPU-browser-tests.log`,
	  `mac-fixed-k20`, Ampere, N=512, 600f, K20, cycle gate on) vs its sl peers
	  (`2026-10-21-04_23..1`, `07_19`, `08_02..1`):
	  - `vel` goes 0.034 -> 0.050 (`blobs`), 0.035 -> 0.054 (`dam`), 0.025/0.031 -> 0.048
	    (`cycle`), 0.029 -> 0.047 ms/substep (`jet`), adding +16..19 us/substep (~1 % of a
	    2-cycle substep). an `rgba32float` scratch carrying trace helpers is therefore
	    closed: 18 us leaves nothing worth doubling scratch bandwidth for.
	  - fps goes 105.1 -> 104.5 (-0.6 %), 105.3 -> 105.0 (-0.3 %), 104.2/106.5 -> 106.1,
	    157.6 -> 158.6 (+0.6 %), clearing the <= 15 % equal-ns bar by >10x.
	  - conservation is `[ok]` on every channel, end `over=0` everywhere, and settled
	    `true` residual is as good or better (3.89e-5, 1.68e-4, 7.19e-3, 2.97e-3).
	`cfg.advectMac` stays 0: the auto-policy capture (`2026-10-23-05_58`)
	measures 67.3 / 57.6 / 61.3 / 79.2 fps (blobs/dam/cycle/jet), failing
	the all-scenario >= 60 fps gate on dam. settled ns rises from 6/7/12/12
	to 8/9/15/13; equal-ns kernel cost does not predict auto-policy frame cost.
	Reconsider adoption only after dam holds >= 60 fps without lowering quality.
	vorticity confinement is a force, not an advection change: F = eps*h*(N x omega)
	with eps in 1/s and N the unit gradient of |omega|. omega is dv/dx - du/dy at
	the cell centre from the neighbouring cell-centred velocities (the average of
	the four corner curls); (vT-vB)-(uR-uL) is dv/dy-du/dx, which is 0 for
	solid-body rotation and for pure shear, and the oracle asserts those fixed
	points plus the gaussian core value 2*A/sigma. measured on the cpu oracle,
	on the same stirred vortex as the advection rows
	(`experiments/logs/2026-10-22-vortex-decay-conf.log`, N=128, 4 s, ranked on
	errL2 because every eps overshoots peak vorticity past its initial value):
	- eps=0.5 is the gentle row: errL2 0.274 -> 0.233, KE keep 0.563 -> 0.607,
	  maxDiv unchanged (5.04e-3), peak vorticity keep 1.28, the clamp at 0.
	- eps=1 costs no divergence either (5.04e-3) and improves errL2 to 0.201,
	  KE keep to 0.660, enstrophy keep 0.476 -> 0.672.
	- eps=2 is the errL2 optimum: 0.161, KE keep 0.759, enstrophy keep 0.934,
	  for 1.1x the divergence (5.55e-3). peak vorticity keep 1.96 is the price
	  in shape: the core is nearly twice as intense as the vortex started.
	- eps=4 and eps=8 are over-driven: errL2 turns back up (0.173, 0.259),
	  maxDiv 3.1x / 15x, enstrophy keep 1.90 / 4.60 and the peak speed passes
	  its initial value (84, 101 against 80). rejected.
	- cost: two extra passes over the velocity field. on the cpu the force slice
	  goes 4.0 -> ~12.4 ms per step at N=128 (the centred curl re-reads blocked
	  predicates per tap; the wgsl form is two texture passes), against a step
	  that is mostly projection.
	re-ranked stacked on mac-cormack (`experiments/logs/2026-10-23-vortex-decay-mac-conf.log`,
	`node experiments/vortex-decay.js --eps none --mac-eps 0.25,0.5,1,2`, N=128, 4 s):
	- mac alone gives errL2 0.0855, KE keep 0.874, vort keep 0.783, maxDiv 3.20e-3,
	  max speed 80.00, macLimiter 17,349.
	- the sl picks eps=1 and eps=2 make errL2 worse than mac alone (0.1096 and
	  0.2312), inflate maxDiv 6.7x / 20x (2.15e-2 / 6.41e-2), push max speed above
	  the initial 80 (84.19 / 95.54), and trip macLimiter 44x / 86x (757k / 1.50M).
	- eps=0.5 still overshoots initial max speed (80.63 at 3 s), spins peak
	  vorticity to 2.20x initial, and raises maxDiv 5.7x (1.83e-2) for a negligible
	  errL2 move (0.0855 -> 0.0817).
	- eps=0.25 spins core vorticity to 1.81x initial and raises maxDiv 3.3x
	  (1.05e-2) for -0.023 errL2 (0.0855 -> 0.0623, vs -0.189 from sl -> mac).
	rejected on the mac track (t17): no wgsl port. `cfg.vorticityConfinement`
	stays 0 in the cpu solver as an opt-in experiment knob.

### 4.3 formats and bandwidth

	conserved (mass, therm): stay rgba32float. non negotiable.
	candidates for rgba16float/rg16float: vel, press, divsrc, sfield, flux, limiter.
	decision: adopt per field only if drift_rel and the residual are unchanged at the
	baseline tolerance and frame time improves. flux is the risky one: a half precision
	flux still conserves exactly (both cells read the same stored half value) but changes
	the transported amount, so it is allowed; press affects only the solve quality.
	pass fusion: in the 0.5.0+ graph `derive_T` is already part of `phaseEos`, and
	`freezeVel` runs twice inside the phase chain after transport, while `trans`
	(0.11-0.12 ms/substep) + `phase` (0.13-0.15 ms/substep) account for ~85 % of
	non-projection GPU time (`2026-10-23-04_54`). two bit-identical fusions fit inside
	WebGPU core's `maxStorageTexturesPerShaderStage = 4` (`requiredLimits: {}`) with
	flat control flow (`cfg.fusePasses`, `&fuse=1`, presets `fuse-fixed-k20`,
	`fuse-k20`, `mac-fuse-k20`):
	- `advectScalar` fuses `advectMass` + `advectTherm` (`trans` 5 -> 4 dispatches,
	  one shared set of `flux` loads and donor weights, 2 write storage textures).
	- `phaseSources` (`phaseEos` specialized with `override APPLY_SOURCES = 1` over an
	  explicit `phaseLayout`) folds `heatSources` into the second `phaseEos` (`phase`
	  6 -> 5 dispatches, saving an 8.4 MB `therm` read+write per substep at N=512 and
	  duplicate `evalPhase`; `therm` flips 4x/substep instead of 5x).
	verified bit-exact (`maxDiff === 0`) against the unfused WGSL passes in
	`experiments/wgsl-exec.mjs` (`experiments/logs/2026-10-23-wgsl-exec.log`).
	decision: adopt (`cfg.fusePasses = 1`) only when repeated matched device rows save
	> 3 % frame time with unchanged conservation and trajectory. The follow-up Ampere
	captures (`2026-10-23-06_38*`, `06_40`, `06_41`, `06_42`, `06_43`) give fixed-ns
	mean frame-time savings of 1.37/2.02/1.16/2.51 % (blobs/dam/cycle/jet) across two
	runs per setting. The one new auto pair (`06_42` vs `06_43`) gives 0.93/1.32/3.79/
	2.78 %; only cycle clears 3 %, once. Fused and unfused state diagnostics match
	exactly in the matched captures, but fixed runs were grouped by setting and the
	browser FPS includes all 600 frames (no explicit warm-up discard). Keep default 0.
	The proposed warm-up/discard and interleaved auto cycle A/B is deferred to
	`deferred/0.6.0-t8-cycle-fusion-validation-plan.md`; do not spend the next
	optimization cycle on this low-confidence gain.
	ping pong reduction: `divsrc` is already a single texture (`pool.divsrc`). `sfield`
	(`rgba32float`) cannot drop `sfieldTmp` while `phaseEos` reads `prevS` to charge
	`latentChargedE`, because WebGPU core forbids `read_write` storage access on
	`rgba32float`.
	memory side (informational; `node experiments/gpu-memory.js [--whatif]`): the pool is
	28 textures and 8 buffers (the mac-cormack scratch is the 28th), 54.9 MB logical at
	N=512 and 217.7 MB at N=1024 in the allocation estimate. Candidate format maps may lower this, but footprint alone does not
	justify a change; only measured speedups that preserve quality do.

### 4.4 dispatch and occupancy

	workgroup size sweep: 8x8, 16x8, 16x16, 32x8 (16x16 = 256 is the core limit, 8x8 = 64
	is the compatibility mode safe choice). stencil kernels often prefer 16x8 or 8x8 because
	of the shared texture cache line layout.
	early return cost: kernels that discard out of range invocations still occupy slots;
	for ghost padded grids, dispatch exactly the interior size and offset the index instead.
	one compute pass vs several: measure whether splitting helps (it usually does not, but
	the driver barrier behaviour varies).

### 4.5 automatic quality

	a controller that reads the smoothed frame ms and adjusts, in this order:
		projection iteration count, fct on/off, grid N (with a full realloc, hysteresis
		of at least 2 s), substep cap
	target band 12-16 ms. off by default (developers want fixed settings), on for the
	integration build (0.7.0). must never touch dt in a way that changes conservation, and
	must log every change so a quality drop is explainable.

### 4.6 readback and display

	readbacks: the stats reduction and the buoy sample both end in `mapAsync`. a skipped
	sample is not a correctness bug (the body holds its last force), but it becomes a
	behaviour change under load. variants: current single-slot, a 2-3 entry MAP_READ ring,
	or one copy that carries stats + buoy. decision: adopt the one that
	keeps the readback out of the frame trace at 60 fps with the smallest extra memory.
	when 0.7.0 adds the probe table, its readback shares this path rather than becoming a
	third copy. any ring needs the same generation guard that stats and buoy already use,
	so a readback that lands after reset or resize is dropped.
	display: variants are the current N-sized rgba16float display with a fullscreen
	render, a display texture sized to the css pixels (filtered upscale from N), and a
	reduced dispatch that writes every other cell. decision: adopt only if the pooled
	image SSIM/diff against the current view is acceptable and the pass timing improves;
	the numbers on screen must not change because of a view optimization.
	frame pacing: report p50, p95 and 1 % low separately. a 60 fps average with a 30 fps
	1 % low reads as a stutter, not a success.

## 7. risks and pitfalls

- optimizing before the baseline exists: everything in this plan is invalid without t2.
- half precision creep into the conserved path "just for one field" — the ledger will not
	catch it immediately, so the rule is written in 0.0.0 section 6 and repeated here.
- multigrid with a variable coefficient and huge density ratios can converge worse than
	red-black on the coarse levels; use a coefficient aware restriction (harmonic mean) and
	verify on the dam break scenario, not on a uniform box.
- mac-cormack overshoot without a limiter looks like sparkling noise at interfaces.
- vorticity confinement amplifies whatever vorticity is there, so the strength
  that sharpens a clean vortex is not the strength that is safe in boiling flow.
  it is also an energy source that concentrates rotation into the core: every
  eps on the oracle overshoots peak vorticity past its initial value, so rank
  it on the profile error and read vorticity/enstrophy keeps above 1 as
  distortion the error was bought with, not as retention.
- a derivative the solver and its oracle both spell out can be wrong in both
  places and still agree (the first curl here was dv/dy-du/dx, in the force and
  in the metric). keep one definition and pin it to analytic fixed points —
  solid-body rotation, pure shear, the gaussian core value — where no scheme
  is involved.
- pass fusion tends to produce deeply nested kernels, which AGENTS.md forbids; if a fused
	kernel needs more than two nesting levels, do not fuse it.
- timestamp-query is an optional feature: never make the sim depend on it.
- frame time measurements without a warmed up pipeline and without vsync accounted for are
	noise. measure over 300 frames, discard the first 60, report median, p95 and 1 % low.
- an unpinned substep policy silently rewrites the baseline: the same solver at 4 and 8
	substeps differs by ~2x. every before/after pair names the policy and the observed ns.
- ranking a boiling solver by the sampled poissonTrue peak from a capture dated before
	the solve/drift split. that peak is coefficient drift and grows with pressure amplitude.
- readback stalls look like sim cost in a submit-time measurement. keep GPU timestamp
	numbers and wall-clock submit numbers in separate columns.
