The view row under the canvas switches modes on click; on a mouse or trackpad, hovering a mode switches it too.

Shortcuts leave focused form controls alone; click the canvas to use simulation keys again.

Drag to stir and paint. Dye painting is ledger-accounted: drift stays green. Walls block flow and are visible in view 5.

Pick the convection scenario (or press n until it appears) for heated-from-below rolls: it starts hot at the bottom and cold at the top and switches to the temperature view. Other scenarios start at uniform ambient temperature, so their temperature map is flat by construction.

The dam scenario drops a buoy: an outlined rectangle lifted by the blue inside it, so it rides the water surface. It only reads the field, so nothing is added to the ledger. u switches between auto-cfl substeps and the fixed step count in the panel.

Drift thresholds: green < 1e-6, yellow < 1e-3, red above. min values are the monotonicity watchdog.
