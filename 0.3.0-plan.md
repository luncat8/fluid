# 0.3.0 — milestone 1b: conserved color transport

status: planned
depends on: 0.2.0, 0.1.1 (cpu reference for advection)
milestone: draft.txt milestone 1 ("visualization show color properly not/mix",
"strict keep mass (value) conservation, avoid rounding or other loss")

## 1. goal

three color tracers plus substance mass plus internal energy, all transported by one
face volume flux that is computed exactly once per face per step, so every conserved
quantity is conserved discretely by construction. colors stay visibly distinct
(r next to g reads as r and g, and only the overlap reads as yellow), no negative
values, and the hud shows the drift of all five invariants with numbers that a
regression cannot hide behind.

## 2. scope

in: compute_flux, advect_scalar (donor cell upwind for m, dr, dg, db, E), dye
emitters, the source ledger, boundary loss accounting, conservation meters, color
view mode, sharpness measurement, gpu/cpu parity for transport.

out: fct flux limiting (0.3.1), energy sources and conduction (0.4.0), phase (0.5.0).
E is transported here but nothing changes it yet beyond advection.

## 3. design

### 3.1 compute_flux

	fx(i,j) = u(i,j) * dt_sub      left face of cell (i,j)
	fy(i,j) = v(i,j) * dt_sub      bottom face of cell (i,j)
	fx,fy = 0 when either neighbour is static solid, frozen solid, or outside the
	interior (domain boundary faces)
	fx,fy clamped to +-flux_max = 1 cell volume per substep (the cfl guard of 0.2.0)
one texture (rg32float), written once, read by both adjacent cells in advect_scalar.
this single write is the whole conservation argument: identical float, opposite sign.

### 3.2 advect_scalar

one kernel, one workgroup grid, transports the packed fields:

	mass.r = dr, mass.g = dg, mass.b = db, mass.a = m
	therm.r = E   (therm.g/b/a are written by phase passes later, carried through here)

for each of the 4 faces of the cell:
	upwind cell = the neighbour the flux comes from (sign of the flux decides)
	transferred = flux * q_upwind
	q_new = q + inflow_from_left_and_bottom - outflow_to_right_and_top

implemented as four adds per quantity, all reading the same flux texture, all writing
to the ping pong destination. donor cell upwind: monotone, positive preserving,
exactly conservative, first order (diffusive; 0.3.1 fixes the diffusion without
touching conservation).

### 3.3 why not semi-lagrangian here

semi-lagrangian samples with interpolation, which is a weighted average: it moves
mass between cells that the flux never crossed, and every step loses a little of the
peak. over 1000 steps a dye blob both fades and spreads, and the total is not
reproducible. draft.txt explicitly forbids this ("avoid rounding or other loss"), so
conserved quantities use fluxes. velocity keeps semi-lagrangian because momentum is
not a tracked invariant and stability there matters more.

### 3.4 source ledger

params carries counters that the emitters add to (on the cpu side, per step):

	injected_m, injected_dr, injected_dg, injected_db, injected_E
	removed_* (sinks, drains), boundary_loss_* (open edges)
expected_total = initial_total + injected - removed - boundary_loss
drift_abs = actual_total - expected_total
drift_rel = drift_abs / max(expected_total, eps)

the initial totals are captured at scenario load into stats and never recomputed from
the live field (recomputing would hide a leak that happens on the first step).

open edge loss is measured in compute_flux: fluxes on open boundary faces are added to
boundary_loss (a separate tiny reduction, only when an open edge exists).

### 3.5 emitters

up to 4 slots in params (0.0.0 section 3.3): position, radius, kind, value, dye rgb.
kinds in this plan: dye (adds mass with a color, no energy), drain (removes mass,
charged to removed_*), and momentum (from 0.2.0). painting with the pointer uses the
selected emitter slot, so the user can draw r, g or b directly and watch them meet.

an emitter adds mass to cells weighted by the falloff, and the added amount is
computed on the cpu (sum of weights is known analytically for the falloff used) or
measured by a reduction; the simpler and exact option is: cpu computes the per cell
weights table once per emitter configuration into a small staging buffer, uploads it,
and adds exactly the same numbers to the ledger. no approximation, no drift.

### 3.6 color view mode

	display.rgb = clamp(dye * color_gain, 0, 1)
	display.a = 1
no color space conversion, no tonemap: r+g must read as yellow on screen exactly as
the numbers say. the canvas format from getPreferredCanvasFormat is bgra8unorm, so the
fragment shader writes (b, g, r, a) — swizzle once, in one place, with a comment.
background: cells with m < m_eps render as a dark neutral so vacuum is visible.

### 3.7 stats reduction additions

	totals: m, dr, dg, db, E
	mins: m, dr, dg, db, E           (monotonicity watchdog)
	counts: cells with negative m or dye, cells with m > rho_liq*1.001 (overpacked)
	boundary loss accumulators
two level reduction, one pass over all fields at once (each workgroup accumulates a
fixed struct in shared memory, writes one slot to stats_part).

### 3.8 hud conservation block

per invariant: total, expected, drift_rel (colored), min value, negative cell count.
plus a "since reset" step counter so a drift number always has a time base.
key s dumps the whole stats buffer to the console as a copy pasteable line for the log.

## 5. acceptance

- a1 three blobs stirred together: overlap regions read as yellow/cyan/magenta, blob
	cores stay saturated, no uniform brown after 1000 steps at N=256.
- a2 closed box, no emitters: drift_rel of m, dr, dg, db, E stays at the f32 storage
	floor: every cell update rounds to f32, so the total is a random walk at ~eps32
	scale. measured floor: 3.7e-8 relative over 1200 stirred frames (conformance
	2026-10-18); the bar is 1e-7. a masked-face inconsistency shows up orders of
	magnitude above this and is a bug, not rounding.
- a3 with emitters and drains: drift_rel below 1e-6 for all five invariants.
- a4 negative cell count is 0 for the whole run; min(m), min(dye) >= 0.
- a5 no cell exceeds m > rho_liq * 1.001 in a liquid scenario (overpacking watchdog).
- a6 open edge: boundary_loss matches the missing mass exactly (drift_rel below 1e-6).
- a7 sharpness baseline: with constant face flux F=0.5 at N=256 after 240 steps,
	upwind front width is 26 cells (5%-95% of the dye jump). 0.3.1 compares against
	this same test and requires at least 3x narrower FCT width.
- a8 gpu/cpu parity below 1e-6 relative after 500 steps of the stirred three blob
	scenario.
- a9 60 fps at N=256, >= 30 fps at N=512 with the transport passes included.

## 6. risks and pitfalls

- the single write rule: any "optimization" that recomputes a face flux inside
	advect_scalar (for example sampling velocity directly instead of the flux texture)
	breaks exact conservation. comment it at the kernel head.
- masked faces must be masked identically in compute_flux, freeze_solid and boundary,
	otherwise a face leaks mass into a solid.
- upwind picks the donor by the sign of the flux; a sign error shows up as anti
	diffusion (values explode) — the min/max watchdog catches it in one frame.
- float16 anywhere in the conserved path: forbidden. if memory becomes an issue at
	N=1024, reduce the grid, not the precision (0.6.0 discusses what may be f16).
- summation order in the reduction differs from the cpu linear sum; compare drift, not
	absolute totals, when doing parity.
- emitter accounting must use the same numbers the gpu adds. a cpu computed analytic
	sum that differs from the uploaded weight table by 1 ulp shows up as drift; upload
	the table and sum the table.
- substepping: the ledger accumulates per substep, and stats_every counts frames, not
	substeps. keep the two counters separate in the hud.
- E advection uses upwind on E itself (not on T). advecting T and recomputing E would
	lose energy wherever two cells of different heat capacity mix.
